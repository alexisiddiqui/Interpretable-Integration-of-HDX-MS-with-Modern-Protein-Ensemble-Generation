## MoPrP

The basin, paths, MSM and flow outputs have been visually verified.

- `moprp_basins_main` places the six MI+PB seed circles beside the same twelve representative frames per circle. The six structural views share one camera. The pH 7 minimised reference and its ensemble are rigidly aligned to the pH 4 reference using Cα atoms in PrP 175–187 and 201–216. The original per-frame fits and per-residue backbone-RMSD B factors are retained. Labels mark β1, α1, β2 and the core on the reference, rather than on a selected displaced frame. Colours retain the 0.5–8 Å range.
- `moprp_paths_main` compares Folded starts, core-distorted-lobe starts and detached-β1–α1-set starts at both run pH values. Every trajectory in each selected group is included. An extension replaces its own short prefix, so no run is counted twice. Each pH row contains 24 Folded-start runs (12 × 20 ns and 12 × 100 ns), 12 lobe-start runs and 12 detached-set-start runs (both 100 ns). Lines are 1 ns running means, circles mark starts and squares mark ends. Seed origin is stated separately from run pH. The complete source-basin grid on both planes remains in `../biofeat-moprp-paths-ext12/seeded_paths_ext12_{pca,dih}.png` for the appendix.
- `moprp_msm_main` uses the stored prior weights from the two-constraint segment-MaxCal archive. Its prior maximum-likelihood populations and macrostate free energies are exactly equal to those of the original main MSM at both pH values. Only the prior weights enter this figure. The original 60 × 60 bins, ranges, kT and 0–6 kcal/mol colour scale are retained. The star marks the discussed arm position, PC (87.5, 17.5). Its map height is a projected bin free energy relative to that map's minimum; the candidate opening free energies quoted below the maps integrate structural-state weights. They are different quantities. `moprp_audit.json` records the plotted bin boundaries and values, which need not equal values calculated using a different regional binning.
- `moprp_flow_main` retains the existing drift calculation, thresholds, smoothing, speed normalisation and contour/arrow encoding. The region identifiers, key, panel titles and typography change; the drift calculation does not. The shared identifiers 4.1–4.3 and 7.1–7.3 identify seed circles, not experimental PUF assignments. The original early/late comparison remains in `../biofeat-moprp-flow-ext12/`.

The inputs are the local Coulson mirrors `~/Coulson-Chemistry/moprp_basins/` and `~/Coulson-Chemistry/moprp_seeded/`. Their hashes and exact source paths are in `source/input_manifest.json`. The selected stationary weights are from `msm/ext12seg2_q0.5_cartpca10_all-kmall_k100_lag5/msm_weights.npz`; the comparison report is `msm/ext12_cartpca10_all-kmall_k100_lag5/msm_report.json`. Candidate opening values come from `kb/experiments/bf-moprp.md` and the chapter hub. The code in `source/flow_map_original.py` is an unchanged copy of `../biofeat-moprp-flow-ext12/source/flow_map.py`.

## TeaA

- `teaa_main` restores the original side-by-side PC1 and hinge-angle distributions. `teaa_geometry_main` is an identical copy at the previous review filename. The curves show the MD 40:60 target, unguided Boltz, uniform bias union, uniform refined pool and refined MaxEnt weights. Dotted lines mark the MD open and closed reference positions. Gaussian bandwidth is 1.5 Å for PC1 and 1.5° for θ, with logarithmic density axes as in the original figure. The PCA log-density redesign and structural inset are no longer used.
- `teaa_coverage_main` is a supporting four-panel comparison of coverage, fitted hinge distributions, state weights and PC1. State bars use θ < −5° for open and θ > +5° for closed, with the intermediate weight retained. The overlapping titles were caused by retaining a centred title while adding a left-aligned title. All title positions are cleared before composing the supporting figure.
- `teaa_fit_support` retains the mixture-fit curve as a separate supporting figure. The curve is read from the original Fiedler scoring logs in `source/` (`.txt` extensions). The 20 open and 20 closed in-state structures are Boltz structures, uniformly weighted within each pool, distinct from the MD frames used to construct the synthetic target. The refined, union and control floors are 0.00268, 0.01083 and 0.01098.

Numerical input is `../biofeat-teaa-landscape-F/per_structure.csv`. All densities retain their original weights and normalisation. Refined state weights are 0.419475 / 0.154539 / 0.425986; control weights are 0.356716 / 0.408388 / 0.234896. Assertions check these against the reported values. Job 185936 completed on Pegasus, and the PNG outputs were downloaded and visually inspected for title overlap, clipping and legend readability. Matching PDF files are provided.

The abandoned structural-inset job failed because the staged PDB files were links to Isambard paths, not because of rendering software. A dereferenced archive was subsequently retrieved, but structural rendering is not required by the revised TeaA composition.
