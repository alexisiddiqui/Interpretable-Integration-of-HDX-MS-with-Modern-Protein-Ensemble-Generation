Run4, hotspot augmentation and dRSA weighting on PINDER development validation. (A) Average precision (AP) for contacts within 10 Å among 18,956,558 cross-chain surface-residue pairs. (B) Accuracy of ranking the supplied antibody/antigen partner above a same-stratum swapped partner, over 645 comparisons from 129 complexes. (C) Binding-induced ΔpKa MAE with bonded-Cys sites excluded. Hmask and dRSA use the fixed 29,305-site evaluation recorded in the model summary; run4 uses the corresponding saved residue audit. All models use the 50% training pool and unmasked free-branch evaluation, with partners embedded separately at their bound conformations. Points show seed 17 for run4 and seeds 17, 29 and 43 for hmask and dRSA. Short bars and numerical labels show arithmetic means; thin lines join the same seed across recipes. Checkpoints were selected by validation state MAE plus interface binding-shift MAE. The run4-to-hmask comparison changes within-complex ranking, negative training and hotspot augmentation together.
