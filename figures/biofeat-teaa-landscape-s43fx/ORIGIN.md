# biofeat-teaa-landscape-s43fx

Reference figure set for the corrected TeaA metadiffusion result (seed 43 rerun with BioFeaturisers `39b8069`, the fixed synthetic amide H). It is not planned as a thesis figure. It replaces `biofeat-teaa-landscape-s43` as the seed-43 reference; that folder is kept because its weights come from the uncorrected model (BioFeaturisers `43cc194`).

- Panels and encodings are those of `biofeat-teaa-landscape-s43/ORIGIN.md` (`pca_logdensity`, `rmsd_lobeII`, `rmsd_allCA`, `summary_1d`, `summary.csv`, `per_structure.csv`). The structures in the unguided, metadiffusion-union, control and in-state panels are unchanged. The refined panels are the rerun refinement (234 structures, all above pLDDT 0.7, against 233 before). Every MaxEnt panel carries weights fitted against the regenerated target.
