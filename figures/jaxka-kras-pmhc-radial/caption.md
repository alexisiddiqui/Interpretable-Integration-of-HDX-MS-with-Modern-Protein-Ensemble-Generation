# KRAS pMHC interface comparison

Mutation-centred radial maps compare the enhanced JDIa41b1 TCR in the experimental G12D and WT complexes (columns 1 and 2, 7OW6 and 7OW5) with the screened ATLAS lead ID_1457932 in its predicted G12D and WT complexes (columns 3 and 4). Each panel shows partner residues contacting the complete pMHC target within 4.5 Å. The HLA groove defines a common orthographic frame, with the peptide N-to-C direction running left to right and the observed P6 Cα at the origin. Rings are spaced by 5 Å within a 25 Å window. Teal marks the peptide trace, pink marks the mutation site, and gold outlines residues belonging to the original screened patch. Residue footprints are convex envelopes of projected buried solvent-accessible surface samples.

The upper row (A–D) colours the isolated partner's potential in its bound conformation, calculated using PDB2PQR/PROPKA and APBS at pH 7 and 150 mM monovalent salt. Red and blue denote negative and positive potential on a common −5 to +5 kT/e scale. Target charges are excluded. The lower row (E–H) shows Kyte–Doolittle hydrophobicity on a −4.5 to +4.5 scale. Separate property figures and the additional height and side-chain size comparisons are retained in the same folder. Colour scales are fixed across structures and saturate at their limits.

The predicted lead has full-interface iPTM of 0.875 with G12D and 0.761 with WT, with four and one partner residues contacting P6, respectively. These maps describe the saved poses and their local physicochemical environments. The predicted preference does not establish experimental binding selectivity. Projected footprint area is not buried SASA.
