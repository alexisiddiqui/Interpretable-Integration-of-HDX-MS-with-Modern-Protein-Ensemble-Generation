Noise-averaged hmask GQT contact-head response on intact KRAS pMHC assemblies. G12D, matched WT and peptide-free MHC are shown as whole assemblies (A–C) and from the peptide groove (D–F), with one colour scale for the HLA and β2m surfaces across all six panels. The peptide is shown as opaque sticks on the same colour scale, with position 6 labelled. The dedicated groove figure also gives the response for each peptide residue beneath its structural panel. Close-ups look obliquely into the groove with the peptide N-to-C direction from left to right. β2m and the lower HLA domain are hidden in these views, and the HLA surface is partially transparent to expose the peptide. The inputs retain 7OW6 HLA and β2m coordinates. The WT control replaces peptide Asp6 with Gly by side-chain deletion; the peptide-free control removes the peptide at fixed coordinates. Each assembly is embedded with its internal interactions retained. Surface colours show the mean sigmoid contact-head output against 32,768 independent standard Gaussian vectors passed through the checkpoint's learned edge normalisation. Identical noise draws are used for all states. This derived readout measures the response to random embeddings outside the model's training distribution, rather than a calibrated probability of binding.

The companion difference figure shows G12D minus each matched control on shared residues. Red denotes a higher response in G12D and blue a lower response. The peptide is hidden in the deletion comparison because there is no corresponding residue in peptide-free MHC. Peptide contact-head scores remain in the accompanying residue tables. Both panels use one symmetric colour scale.
