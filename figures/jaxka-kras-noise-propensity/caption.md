Noise-averaged hmask GQT contact-head response on intact KRAS pMHC assemblies. G12D, matched WT and peptide-free MHC are shown as whole assemblies (A–C) and from the peptide groove (D–F), with one colour scale across all six panels. The inputs retain 7OW6 HLA and β2m coordinates. The WT control replaces peptide Asp6 with Gly by side-chain deletion; the peptide-free control removes the peptide at fixed coordinates. Each assembly is embedded with its internal interactions retained. Per-residue colours show the mean sigmoid contact-head output against 32,768 independent standard Gaussian vectors passed through the checkpoint's learned edge normalisation. Identical noise draws are used for all states. This derived readout measures the response to random embeddings outside the model's training distribution, rather than a calibrated probability of binding.

The companion difference figure shows G12D minus each matched control on shared residues. Red denotes a higher response in G12D and blue a lower response. The peptide is grey in the deletion comparison because there is no corresponding residue in peptide-free MHC. Both panels use one symmetric colour scale.
