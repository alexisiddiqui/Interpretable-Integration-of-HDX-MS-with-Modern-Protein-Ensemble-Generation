# biofeat-teaa-landscape-F

Figure set for the TeaA result reported in chapter 5, the Fiedler-lobe run (corrected amide H, BioFeaturisers `39b8069`). The two hinge bodies come from the Fiedler vector of Boltz's pair representation, not from a hand split, so no input to generation is chosen by hand. It replaces `biofeat-teaa-landscape-s43fx` as the TeaA figure, because the refinement runs behind that set were deleted and their same-settings regeneration scores differently (`source/s43fx_regeneration_REPORT.md`). Protocol in `kb/experiments/bf-teaa.md` §6.

- `summary_1d`: PC1 and blind θ densities for the MD 40:60 mixture, unguided Boltz (150), the metadiffusion union (550, uniform), and the refined pool (236) uniform and after MaxEnt λ 1000. θ uses the Fiedler axis and lobes (`blind_axis_fiedler.json`; open reference −18.4°, closed +9.7°).
- `pca_logdensity`: Cα PCA fitted on the MD open and closed frames with equal state weighting, log10 density, dashed MD 95 % regions. Twelve panels as in `biofeat-teaa-landscape-s43`, with the Fiedler union, refined and control pools.
- `rmsd_lobeII`, `rmsd_allCA`: RMSD-to-open against RMSD-to-closed for the same panels. The lobe-II RMSD here uses the hand lobes, as in the weight files and `hinge_eval.py`; it is a diagnostic and does not define θ.
- `refmix_curve`: uptake MSE against the target for mixtures of the 20 open and 20 closed in-state pools (`TeaA_inState`, uniform inside each pool), against open fraction, with the λ 1000 MaxEnt floors of the refined pool (0.0027), the metadiffusion union (0.0108) and the control (0.0110). The grid and floors are read from the Fiedler scoring logs (`F_bins_8050284`, `F_blind_8050281`, `F_metad_8049979`, `F_ctl_8050283`).
- `summary.csv`, `per_structure.csv`: per-panel means and state fractions, and per-structure PC1, PC2, RMSDs, θ and λ 1000 weight.
- Source machine: Isambard-AI. Outputs in `~/_runtime/analysis/fiedler/landscapes_F/`, made on 2026-11-09 on the login node with the MaxENT-FK virtual environment (`~/MaxENT-FK/.venv/bin/python`). The weights matched 550/550, 236/236 and 236/236 structures (`source/landscape_F.txt`).
- Code: `source/landscape_plots_F.py` is `~/_runtime/analysis/landscape_plots_s43fx.py` with the pools, weight files, axis JSON, θ lobes and titles changed by `source/make_landscape_F.py` (nothing else). `source/refmix_curve_F.py` draws the mixture curve. `source/fiedler_NOTES.md` is the campaign record on Isambard (lobes, axis, stage-1 chain, stage 2 and scoring job ids).
- SHA-256 prefixes: `b024cfb1` summary_1d, `d3001b9a` pca_logdensity, `0b1f208e` refmix_curve, `ce686d80` rmsd_lobeII, `3e4d9873` rmsd_allCA.
