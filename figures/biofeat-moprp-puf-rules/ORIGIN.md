# biofeat-moprp-puf-rules

Reference figure set projecting PUF rules derived from Moulick et al. 2015 onto the MoPrP PCA and dihedral planes, to decide what each sampled region is. It is not yet planned as a thesis figure. Reading in `kb/experiments/bf-moprp.md`.

- `moprp_puf_maps`: bin means of the paper-derived variables on the PCA101 plane and the dihedral plane, runs at pH 4 and pH 7. Blue rows are native Cα contact fractions relative to Folded (`macro_structure.py` definitions); orange rows are core-fit RMSD. Black contours are MSM free energy (1, 2, 3 kcal/mol, no MaxCal).
- `moprp_puf_rules`: fraction of frames in each bin that meet each rule. A segment is displaced beyond the 99th percentile of Folded frames, pooled over pH.
- Cluster figures: one identity per frame from the same rules, by priority (PUF2 candidate, PUF1 candidate, β1 alone, β1 + partial; then with β1 kept, core distorted, α3 C off, unit off; else Folded-like). pH 4 is the target; pH 7 is shown for comparison and its minimum is not used to define a cluster. Candidate and β1-alone hues are palette slots 1–3, validated all-pairs; the other clusters are neutral.
  - `moprp_puf_clusters_overview`: dominant cluster per bin, by frames and by MSM weight, both pH.
  - `moprp_puf_clusters_candidates`: share of each bin held by the PUF1 and PUF2 candidates, both pH.
  - `moprp_puf_clusters_rules`: share of each bin held by the remaining clusters, both pH.
- MaxCal C figures (run on Coulson `comp1400`, jobs 780861 and 780864): `moprp_puf_c_lag5_heatmap` gives native contacts and core-fit displacement per cluster, in the `macro_structure.py` layout, with ΔG from the MSM and after C at pH 4 and from the MSM at pH 7. `moprp_puf_c_lag5_fes` gives free energy before and after C at pH 4 and the MSM at pH 7, in the `fes_maxcal_plots.py` layout. The `lag2` files are the same at lag 2 ns.
