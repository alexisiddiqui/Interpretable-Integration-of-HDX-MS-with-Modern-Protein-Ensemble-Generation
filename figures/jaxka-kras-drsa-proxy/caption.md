Noise-based burial-change proxy on intact KRAS pMHC assemblies. Blue denotes the residue-wise product of predicted exposure from the hmask burial head and the noise-averaged hmask contact response. The HLA surface and peptide sticks share one colour scale across G12D, matched WT and peptide-free MHC. The view looks into the groove, with the peptide N-to-C direction from left to right and position 6 labelled. The strips give each peptide residue's score. β2m and the lower HLA domain are hidden for display but retained in the model input.

Predicted exposure is one minus the sigmoid burial-head output for the intact assembly. The contact response averages 32,768 Gaussian noise vectors passed through the learned edge normalisation, with identical draws across states. The WT control replaces Asp6 with Gly by side-chain deletion from 7OW6; the peptide-free control deletes the peptide without relaxing the remaining coordinates. The product represents potential additional burial under the chosen noise reference. It is not an observed RSA difference or a calibrated prediction of binding-induced ΔRSA.
