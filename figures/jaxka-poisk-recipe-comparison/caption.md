POISK binder–decoy discrimination across run 4, hmask and dRSA. Blue denotes independently folded free monomers, using ABodyBuilder2 for Fvs and ESMFold for antigens, on 11,111 complete pairs. Orange denotes free monomers taken from bound conformers and embedded separately, and green denotes Protenix-v2 complex poses, each on 11,115 pairs. Hollow circles, hollow diamonds and filled circles identify run 4, hmask and dRSA, respectively, with lines connecting successive recipes. All three models use seed 17. Pairwise accuracy is the fraction of pairs in which the binder outscores its matched decoy, with ties contributing one half; the dashed line marks chance at 0.5. The dotted and solid vertical references show Boltz-2 and Protenix-v2 ipSAE accuracy on the released 11,247-pair benchmark. Contact sums, maxima and top-50 means aggregate residue-pair contact probabilities over the indicated Fv or CDR rows. In free-monomer modes, predicted burial change is free exposure multiplied by the best predicted contact probability to the partner; in complex mode it is predicted bound minus free burial. Burial-weighted contacts multiply each contact probability by the predicted burial contributions at both ends. The top-50 CDR contact readout increases from 43.7% to 71.2% and 71.3% for apo monomers. These binder–decoy accuracies do not distinguish the antibody-intrinsic sequence signal from recognition of its antigen; the wrong-antigen comparisons are recorded in the POISK experiment note.
