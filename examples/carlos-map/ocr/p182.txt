Moreover, further experiments demonstrate that symmetry-adapted kriging is
consistent with the symmetry during a simulation. We have tested models of methanol
and acetaldehyde that contained examples of only one of its symmetry-equivalent
conformers. The results show that this formulation allows to extrapolate towards
unseen geometries employing only the symmetry group of the molecule. In all
cases, the geometries converge readily to the expected minimum within a negligible
RMSD. On the other hand, the baseline lacking ordinary kriging shows numerous

examples of unphysical behaviour due to the extrapolation collapse.

This advance will be incorporated in the next major version of FFLUX, and prob-

ably become one of the most powerful tools to reduce its computational cost.
