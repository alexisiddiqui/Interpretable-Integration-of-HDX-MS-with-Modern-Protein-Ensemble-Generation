However, our group has explored more sophisticated algorithms to obtain the
global minimum, since the number and size of models that have to be trained rou-
tinely is so large that the number of runs required to ensure that the global mini-
mum has been reached is too expensive computationally. We describe here the Par-
ticle Swarm Optimisation (PSO) method, that is the standard in the FEREBUS code
used in our group. Another algorithm, Differential Evolution (DE) is not presented
by lack of space, and can be found in [196].

The PSO algorithm has a very interesting story; it was initially proposed as a
model for collective behaviour of animals, when the researchers discovered that it

actually had a natural tendency to find the global minimum of a function [197].
