3.4.2 The extrapolation collapse

The other two challenges that we will assess are not related to an improper physi-
cal description, but rather to the inconveniences of the mathematical methods em-
ployed in FFLUX. Unfortunately, the challenge assessed in this section, which we
have named extrapolation collapse after encountering it several times, will actually
lead to an unphysical behaviour that can put in danger the conclusions of the sim-

ulations

The extrapolation collapse is a phenomenon occurring whenever a prediction
is done outside the training range. In general, extrapolation beyond the domain of
applicability of any model is a dangerous move. Attempting to apply a linear model
to non-linear physical phenomena, or employing statistical conclusions outside the
population where they were recorded, is doomed to fail. However, in the case of
FFLUx, the subtleties of Gaussian process regression make extrapolation especially

problematic.
