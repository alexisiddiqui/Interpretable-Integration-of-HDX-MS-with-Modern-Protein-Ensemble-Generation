Figure 4.5: Lewis structure of the Ng molecule. On the left, the structure proposed

To find a suitable explanation, we calculated the delocalization indices of the
EEE and EZE isomers of Ng; these are shown in Figure 4.6 for the HF (left) and
CCSD (right) levels of theory. Our proposed structure is shown in the right side of
Figure 4.5.

The majority of results agree with the picture proposed by Hirshberg et al. The
terminal pairs, N8-N7 and N4-N5, show large delocalization values, close to 2.6 in
HE, what clearly suggest triple terminal bonds. Similarly, the central bond N1-N2
has an evident double bond character, with a HF DI of 1.9, and the N6-N2 and N3-
N1 pairs are single, although the DI around 1.12 suggests that this bond is enriched
by some kind of conjugation.

double bond that is depleted by the same resonance phenomenon that enriches the
N6-N2 and N3-N1 pairs. This suggest an structure where the bonds formerly con-
