The analysis of the dataset is extended by three case studies of exotic bonding
patterns: a molecule of Ng, a compound with planar tetracoordinate carbon, and
an organometallic complex that has been object of considerable discussion in the
literature. In all cases, the view of the delocalization index provides some new
insight in absence of any approximations. Particularly interesting is our proposal
of a new Lewis structure for Ng that disagrees with the view of its discoverers, and
the finding that the bonding in the organometallic complex is different to what a
naive QTAIM analysis would find.

Altogether, these results lead us to conclude that delocalization index is a rea-
sonable bond order indicator that can be suitably applied to a general quantum

chemical calculation.
