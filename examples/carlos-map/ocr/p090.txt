Multipolar electrostatics can seem quite obscure for those not familiar with its
terminology. In Appendix C we provide a complete derivation of multipolar elec-
trostatics employing the Cartesian tensor formalism, as well as the formulae for the
different interactions and interaction tensors up to quadrupole. The conclusion of
this treatment, as simple as it is, must be borne in mind: multipole moments are
nothing more than the coefficients in a Taylor expansion”, and if the latter converges, it

is clear that adding more terms improves the description of the potential.
