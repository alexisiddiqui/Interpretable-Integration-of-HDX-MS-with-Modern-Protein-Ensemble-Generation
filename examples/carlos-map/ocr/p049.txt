Two questions remain to completely determine the theory: which sites are cho-
sen to harbour the multipoles, and which terms are allocated to those sites. The
first question is answered in a rather arbitrary way: the centres are usually atoms,
or small groups of atoms, although sometimes multipole sites are placed at bonds.

The second question is slightly more involved.

Equation 2.75 seems to indicate that multipoles can be displaced freely along
the molecule. In practice, however, displacement by a distance t multiplies every
term in the expansion by t!~*. If the displacement is large, higher multipoles will be
enhanced, creating unnecessary computational expenses and enlarging the sphere
of divergence of the multipole expansion. Therefore it is in the best of interests that

the displacement is minimised as much as possible.
