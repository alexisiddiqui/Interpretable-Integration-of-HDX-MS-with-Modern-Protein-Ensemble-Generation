As will be discussed in Section 3.2, the formulae for the forces in FFLUX depend
on the assumption that the kriging model uses the positions of the molecule as fea-
tures. One could, in principle, employ the coordinates of the molecule in a global
Cartesian frame, but these contain six superfluous degrees of freedom (three of po-
sition and three of rotation) that are not required, and that are a source of errors and
inaccuracy during training. The power of the ALF resides in its ability to eliminate

these degrees of freedom.
