3.1 Architecture of FFLUX

3.1.1 The potential: knowledgeable quantum atoms

The Quantum Chemical Topology Force Field breaches with traditional force field
expressions (harmonic potentials, torsion terms, etc.) by modelling a system in
terms of knowledgeable quantum atoms [20], a set of atomistic kriging models that
try to mimic the properties of quantum atoms (atomic energies and multipolar mo-
ments) by interpolation of previously seen examples. In this way, instead of ’clas-
sicalising” interactions by means of approximate formulas, the system is modelled

fully from a quantum mechanical perspective using non-parametric regression.
