In practice, we require the kernel to also fulfil certain notions of similarity. In

this context, it is usual to quote Tobler’s first law of geography [193]:

Everything is related to everything else, but near things are more related
than distant things.

We note that the generality of the kernel functions has allowed to extend the
Gaussian process formalism towards all kind of areas, including text, graphs, chem-

informatic models and even chemical reactions.
