The main exemplar of inducible dipole force field is AMOEBA (Atomic Multi-

Another example is the NEMO (Non-Empirical MOdel) [134], that employs a
description of the electrostatics very similar to AMOEBA, but including induced

quadrupoles as well as dipoles.

2.4 Quantum Chemical Topology

Since the start of this thesis, we have kept a strong will to connect our line of rea-
soning to the principles of quantum mechanics. However, there is a matter which
has not been addressed yet. Chemistry is built around a handful of very useful con-
cepts: the notions of atoms, bonds, and movements of electrons that allow to ex-
plain an enormous body of knowledge across the chemical sciences. Unfortunately,

connecting these concepts with quantum mechanics is hardly a trivial matter.

The notion of individual or discrete atoms has a long story, starting with the
early insights of Democritus. While the atomic theory had to strive for long before
it was totally accepted (let us note that several biographers have linked the suicide
of Ludwig Boltzmann in 1906 to the resistance of the scientific community to accept

the atomic theory), it is deeply engraved in the scientific thinking.

to the advent of quantum mechanics, and reconcile the discreteness of matter with
wavefunctions has been one of the classical conundrums of theoretical chemistry.
