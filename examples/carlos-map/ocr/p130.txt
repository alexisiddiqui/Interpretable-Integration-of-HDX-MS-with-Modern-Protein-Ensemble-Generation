Since computation of exact values employing the full 2-RDM is computationally
prohibitive for even small molecules, both in CPU time and memory, this bench-
mark sheds light on the errors that might appear when this approach is applied to

moderately sized systems, where it is the only alternative.

4.2.3 Influence of correlation

We now analyse the influence of correlation in the delocalization indices. Previous
work has compared HF calculations with MP2, CISD and DFT [256, 255], and al-
ways in the context of directly bonded atoms; here we add the comparison with
CCSD/Miiller and extend the analysis to non-covalent interactions. Unless other-
wise specified, it will be assumed that CCSD corresponds to the Miiller approxima-
tion of the CCSD 2-RDM.

Unsurprisingly, Hartree-Fock results readily recover the Lewis structure bond
order; examples such as 6(H,H) = 1.000 for H2 and 6(N,N) = 3.043 for N2 show
an almost exact correspondence with those values, while others such as 6(O,O) =
2.272 for Oz are slightly different from the expected error, but still clearly recognis-

able as a particular bond order.
