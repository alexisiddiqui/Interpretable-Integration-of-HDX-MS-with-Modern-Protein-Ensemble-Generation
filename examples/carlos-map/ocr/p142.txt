4.3.3 A discussed organometallic complex

Metallic complexes are notorious for their theoretical difficulties: they are highly
multiconfigurational, and the difficulty to provide a suitable description, together
with the inability of Miiller’s approximation to handle static correlation, has led us
to avoid this class of compounds. In this last section, we will attempt to extend our

approach to an organometallic complex.

The complex studied is (y*-CsH¢)Fe(CO)3, and presents interesting properties
that will ease a theoretical treatment. In the first place, it is an organometallic com-
plex that fulfils the 18-electron rule: therefore, basic inorganic chemistry [298] leads
us to expect that the HOMO-LUMO bandgap will be large, and thus the mixing
coefficients of the single determinant reference and excited determinants shall be
small. In fact, a CASSCF(10, 10) calculations shows that the Hartree-Fock reference
has a coefficient of 0.91, to be compared with an approximate coefficient of 0.8 for
an analogous computation in CH4. This is a strong proof that correlation is mostly

dynamic, and that a CCSD wavefunction can provide an acceptable modelling.
