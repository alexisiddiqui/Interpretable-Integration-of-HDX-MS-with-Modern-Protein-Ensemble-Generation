Chapter 4

Delocalization indices and bond order

HE following chapter is an example of how research often starts at a
given point and evolves in a completely unexpected dimension. Here
we analyse a magnitude from Quantum Chemical Topology, the delo-
calization index, that was initially studied as a possible magnitude to tie together
individual monomer kriging models, and soon acquired a dimension of its own as

an indicator of bond order.

We note that the research discussed in this chapter has recently been published
as an Edge Article in the journal Chemical Science; a copy of the article is provided
in Appendix A. Although this chapter provides a more extensive discussion, along
with some unpublished results and comments that were unfit for the publication,
there are also many features in common, in particular the same figures. We also note
that our! contributions to the paper include the vast majority of the calculations and
the totality of the analysis: it is therefore legitimate to include the contents of the

paper in this thesis.
