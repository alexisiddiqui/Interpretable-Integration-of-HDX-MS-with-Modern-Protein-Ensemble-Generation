4.2.2 Benchmark of Miiller approximation

Several articles have compared Miiller approximation with the full 2-RDM in a va-
riety of contexts [264, 277, 278, 279, 280]. In this section, we provide a short bench-

mark on 21 small molecules, which is provided in Table 4.1

In the original paper, Miiller stated that the complications of his approximation
may be compensated by the mean accuracy of this approximation for large N [273]. In this
sense, it is to be expected that the approximation will become asymptotically better
as the system size increases, and our benchmark can be interpreted as an upper

bound in the error.

The results in Table 4.1 show that Miiller’s approximation work best when cor-
relation plays a minor role on the system. Most of the DIs for directly bonded atoms
show relative errors below 5%, which is more than acceptable in relation to the enor-
mous computational savings. We note that the computation required 2 weeks in a
