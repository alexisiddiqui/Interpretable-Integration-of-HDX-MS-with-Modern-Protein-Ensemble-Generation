The distinguishing feature of Gaussian process regression is that we assume
that the regression function f can be taken as the sample from a Gaussian process:
a stochastic process (i. e. a collection, perhaps infinite, of random variables) in which
every finite subcollection of random variables follow a multivariate normal distri-

bution (the generalisation of a normal distribution to several variables).
