The delocalization indices show that the interaction between the terminal atoms
(which we will denote by BC) and the iron atom is actually stronger than its ana-
logue for the central atom in TMM (which we will denote by aC). Although there is
no direct BC-Fe bond path, 6(BC, Fe) = 0.469 is almost twice as much as 6(aC, Fe) =
0.292. This is emphasised by the CCSD results, where 5(BC,Fe) = 0.788 and
6(aC, Fe) = 0.286.
