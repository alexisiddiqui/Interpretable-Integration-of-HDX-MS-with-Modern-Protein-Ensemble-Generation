Overall, our contributions have provided two major methodological improve-
ments. The first, the delocalization index, will be used as a bond order indicator
for chemical structure and reactivity studies; it might also become an useful quan-
tity for the Quantum Chemical Topology Force Field in the long run. The second,
symmetry-adapted kriging, is ready to be deployed as a tool to fit molecular poten-
tials, and can be incorporated in further versions of FFLUX.
