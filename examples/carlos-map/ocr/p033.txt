2.1.3 Classical mechanics: force fields

Ab initio molecular dynamics provides a complete treatment of the electronic be-
haviour and has been used extensively. Nevertheless, its significant computational
cost has precluded the application to very large systems and/or very large simu-
lation times. This hinders the study of phenomena such as protein folding, or ion

passing through biological channels, of deep scientific interest.

In these systems, most of the effects of quantum mechanics can be reasonably
approximated using simple functional forms that capture the basic physical be-
haviour. For example, bond stretching can accurately be fitted to a Morse potential
