4.1 Theoretical background

4.1.1 Delocalization indices

In order to arrive to the concept of delocalization index in a sensible manner, we
will state our objective and then manipulate the basic tools of quantum chemistry
to arrive at it. Since we want to reconcile modern electronic structure theory with
the Lewis’ picture of chemical bonding, we aim to find an indicator that yields the

amount of delocalized electrons among two atoms.

We will adopt the QTAIM scheme to partition the IR* space in a set of atomic
regions, although we note that any exhaustive partitioning of real space can be
used to calculate delocalization indices. We will also employed the reduced density

matrices of first and second order:
