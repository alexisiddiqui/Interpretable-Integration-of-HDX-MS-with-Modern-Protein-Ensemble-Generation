4.3 Application to exotic bonding patterns

In the previous section we have discussed the ability of delocalization indices
to unravel certain chemical information; however, we have employed only simple,
well-known chemical patterns. The next step is to employ this methodology in a set
of exotic bonding patterns, in an attempt to discover whether they can be employed

as a tool to understand novel chemistry.

4.3.1 The molecule of Ng

The molecule of Ng was found serendipitously by Hirshberg, Gerber and Krylov in
2014 [285]: while trying to study Na crystals, the system converged spontaneously
to a molecular crystal with Ng units. We note that this and similar molecules were
studied by the Gordon group as early as 2000 [286], but the findings of the Krylov
group had much more repercussion because they actually found Ng to be stable.
More importantly, we note that their predictions have been confirmed experimen-

tally very recently [287].
