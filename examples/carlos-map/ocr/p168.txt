5.4.1 Simple models: dissociation curves

As a first test of our methodology, we tested symmetry-adapted kriging in two
very simple models: the dissociation curves of water and ammonia. We devel-
oped a simplified implementation of the symmetry-adapted algorithm in the Molec-
ularSAGPR code, which builds on the scikit-learn Python library [222].

We selected these two molecules due to their simple symmetries and small size.
For example, the symmetry in water is the permutation group P(2), which has two
elements: the identity, and the operation that permutes both hydrogen atoms; note
that this group is isomorphic to the point group C,, as are all groups with two ele-

ments. In the notation that we have established, the representation matrices of this
