5.3.2 Implementation in FFLUX

The algorithm described above was implemented in the last version of DL_POLY
FFLUX [232], with some modifications and corrections. We will outline this imple-
mentation, describing its structure and the logic behind. Moreover, the FORTRAN

code of our routines is provided in Appendix B.

All the operations of the algorithm are implemented in the two_body_forces.f90
file, where previous work incorporated the valence potential of FFLUX [232]. Ef-
fectively, when the FFLUX potential is active (represented by the variable keyfce
being equal to 7), the subroutine evaluates the IQA kriging models and returns the

corresponding derivatives for the forces.

Our approach was conceived to preserve as much of the previous implementa-
tion as possible, and is depicted in Figure 5.4. Instead of applying the symmetry
operations during the prediction model, we modify the Cartesian coordinates to
ensure that the features are in the appropriate symmetry region. This is a done
through a subroutine fflux_symmetry_transformation, that is applied before the pro-

cess of computing the features and then the forces.
