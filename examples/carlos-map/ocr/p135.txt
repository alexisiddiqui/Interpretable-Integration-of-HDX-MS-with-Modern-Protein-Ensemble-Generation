4.2.5 The subtler bonding effects

The delocalization index is sensitive to the environment of the bond it refers, and
in that way it can reflect some well-known effects in practical chemistry. We will
understand the DI to be under the influence of two responses: the atoms that con-

stitute the bond, and their surroundings.
