3.3 The training of kriging models

The last two sections have provided a comprehensive treatment of the archi-
tecture and potentials employed in the Quantum Chemical Topology Force Field
(FFLUX). We shall now complete this description by addressing the parametrization

process of the force field: how are the atomistic kriging models trained.

The details of the training are best specified by presenting the actual parametriza-
tion pipeline. This relies in GAIA, a long Perl script that has been developed in the
Popelier group during the past few years [234, 217]. GAIA automates most of the
operations, interacting with several commercial and in-house codes, and providing

checks to ensure that every step is performed correctly.
