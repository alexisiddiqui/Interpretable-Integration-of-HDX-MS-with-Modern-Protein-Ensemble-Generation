Finally, we note that there is an ongoing discussion in the literature [172, 173,
174, 175, 176] regarding how density functionals seem to provide reasonable en-
ergy differences, but their description of the electron density (and thus the Kohn-
Sham orbitals) might be very poor. We note that, in absence of the exact functional
promised by Hohenberg-Kohn first theorem, there is no guarantee that the Kohn-
Sham orbitals will provide a good description of the electronic structure, and thus
IQA calculations might be completely unreliable. Nonetheless, their use is extended

given the enormous computational expense of ab initio correlated methods.
