Another possibility would be extended sampling: providing a sampling so ex-
tensive that the system will never leave the training set in a reasonable temperature
simulation. However, this brings up several problems. In the first place, this im-
plies intensive computational expenses, both during the training phase and during
simulation. Additionally, bond dissociation is known to be significantly difficult to
model due to multiconfigurational issues, and the methods to tackle this problem
are usually expensive. Finally, a consequence of the curse of dimensionality dis-
cussed vide infra is that the number of sampling points grows exponentially with
sample size, and thus extended sampling is generally impractical and often impos-
sible.

There is no obvious solution, and the extrapolation collapse threatens to be-
come catastrophic, as it is made evident in unpublished results for moderately-
sized molecules. Future work should aim to develop solutions that minimise the

effect on the force field accuracy.
