Attached publication

A.1 Publication in Chemical Science

The results in Chapter 4 have been published in the journal Chemical Science

(Impact factor: 9.063), under the following citation:

C. Outeiral et al.

Revitalizing the concept of bond order through delocalization measures in real
space

Chem. Sci., 2018, 9, 5517-5529

My scientific contributions to this article include the vast majority of the calcu-
lations, and the totality of the analysis. Since I am the first author and have handled
the research directly, the article is reproduced (with some unpublished additions)

in Chapter 4.

I also designed the cover artwork. I thank the contributions from Dr. Matthew
L. Mills (his program Rhorix [328], after some modifications, allowed to draw the
atomic meshes) and Ivan Batalla (his advice allowed to improve significantly the

materials).
