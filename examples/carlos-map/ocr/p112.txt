We first built a list of 200 conformers employing RDKit [245], representing capped
glycine as the SMILES string [246] CC (=0)NC (=0) CNC. These conformations were re-
laxed employing MMFF94 [240] and after a RMSD pruning procedure to discard
geometries that differ by less than 1.0 A from the optimum in [219], the 10 lowest-

energy configurations were selected.

Each of the conformers was then optimised at the B3LYP/apc-1 level of theory
employing ORCA [241] and the RIJCOSX approximation, and the same software
and conditions were employed in 200 fs BOMD simulations at 600 K employing
the Berendsen thermostat. Since the only objective is to distort the conformers, no
equilibration was employed. Finally, the total energies were recalculated employ-
ing GAUSSIAN 09 [235] and the B3LYP/apc-1 level of theory, for consistency with
the original results [219].
