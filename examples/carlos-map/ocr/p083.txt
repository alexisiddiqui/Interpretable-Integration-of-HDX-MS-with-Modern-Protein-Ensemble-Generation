2.5.3 Global optimisation

The field of global optimisation is an active branch of applied mathematics that is
concerned with methods to obtain the global maximum or minimum in a particular
problem. This field is very rich and comprises many methods; here we will only
consider the approaches that are widely used in Gaussian process regression, either

by the community or within the Popelier group.

We note that algorithms for maximising a function can be used identically to
minimise a function, simply by the transformation f’ = —f. Here we will adopt

the convention of minimisation.

The simplest algorithm to find a minimum in a function is the algorithm known
as gradient descent. This algorithm is based in the mathematical fact that the gradi-
ent of the function at a given point is directed towards the direction of maximum
growth of that function. Thus a simple procedure passes by iteratively subtracting

the gradient until a minimum is reached:
