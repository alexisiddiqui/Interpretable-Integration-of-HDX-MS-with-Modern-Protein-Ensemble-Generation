Chapter 5
Symmetry-adapted kriging

HE following chapter will be concerned with developing a methodology
that increases the robustness and efficiency of atomistic kriging mod-
els by implicitly taking into account the symmetries of the system. We
present the algorithm and implement it into FFLUX, showing its capacity to handle

some moderately-sized systems.

This chapter is strongly methodological, being focused in obtaining a consistent
mathematical model as well as an efficient implementation. The mathematical con-
tent of the chapter is intense, and in particular it makes use of finite group theory in
an abstract way. Since the majority of the effort has been dedicated to derivations
and code development, the number of numerical examples will be reduced, as they

are costly and necessary only to verify the framework.

We now turn to the concept of symmetry, which can be loosely defined as the
property of being invariant to certain transformations. This concept is generally un-
derstood in a static, geometrical way, although it is much wider. For example, the
electronic structure of a molecule in vacuum is independent of its position: there is
an invariance upon translation, and thus a form of symmetry. All physical systems
are endowed with certain symmetries, and capturing those in the mathematical
models is key to avoid catastrophes, the symmetry breakings that are well known
in physics.
