This chapter is structured as follows. First, in section 5.1, we review several
approaches for incorporating symmetry into non-parametric potentials. We then

5.1 Related work
5.1.1 Introduction

Including symmetry to improve regression models is hardly a new idea, and has
been analysed in multiple contexts [303, 304]. The following section provides a
brief review of the most important methods present in the literature. Those will be

classified rather roughly in:

e Models that employ symmetry-adapted features, where a permutation of the

coordinates does not change the features.

e Models that are themselves symmetry-adapted, so that a permutation of the

features yields identical results.

The content of this section is extensive, but certainly not comprehensive: provid-
ing a complete description of all the featurization systems is well beyond the scope
of this thesis. Moreover, with the rising popularity of machine learning methods in

molecular simulation, such review would soon become obsolete.
