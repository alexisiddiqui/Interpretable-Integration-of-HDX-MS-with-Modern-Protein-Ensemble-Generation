# Probing the Non-Covalent Structure of Proteins by Amide Hydrogen Exchange and Mass Spectrometry (1997)

## Main point

A perspective on measuring amide hydrogen exchange by acid proteolysis and mass spectrometry. Exchange rates depend on hydrogen bonding and solvent access, so they probe conformation and dynamics; the protection factor compares the computed unstructured rate with the measured folded rate, and the fragment-level MS method resolves exchange to about 1–10 residues.

## What I use from it

The definition of the protection factor as the ratio of the intrinsic (unstructured) to the observed exchange rate, with its assumption about the unfolded state, and the peptide-level resolution of HDX-MS.
