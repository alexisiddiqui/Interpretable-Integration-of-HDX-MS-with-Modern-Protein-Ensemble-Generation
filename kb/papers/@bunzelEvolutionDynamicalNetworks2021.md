# Evolution of Dynamical Networks Enhances Catalysis in a Designer Enzyme (2021)

## Main point

Directed evolution of a designer Kemp eliminase introduced a network of correlated motions in the transition-state ensemble that spans the protein, with remote mutations clustering around it.
