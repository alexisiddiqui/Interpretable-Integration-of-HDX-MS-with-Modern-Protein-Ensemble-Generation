# Prions: Structure, Function, Evolution, and Disease (2024)

## Main point

Mini review (Archives of Microbiology 2025;207:1, published online November 2024) of prion biology. It covers the structure of the cellular prion protein (PrPC) and the scrapie form (PrPSc), the templated conversion that causes prion disease, prion evolution and the potential applications of prions in protein engineering.

## What I use from it

- The conversion cycle and its endpoint in plaque formation (^c1, ^c2), which is panel B of `fig:intro-prion-populations`.
- The ancient origin of prions (^c3). The same passage gives the support: resilience to radiation, heat, crosslinking and enzymatic digestion, and autocatalytic assembly. Its prion-first hypothesis, that prion-like mechanisms acted in early biochemical evolution before DNA and protein-based life, is a separate hypothesis in the same passage.

## Disagreements / limitations

- A review, so its statements on prion evolution rest on the cited primary studies (Jheeta et al. 2021; Zajkowski et al. 2021).
