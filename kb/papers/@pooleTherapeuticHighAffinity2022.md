# Therapeutic high affinity T cell receptor targeting a KRASG12D cancer neoantigen (2022)

## Main point

The study develops an affinity-enhanced TCR recognising the KRAS G12D decamer presented by HLA-A*11:01. Its structural comparison provides the target rationale for the thesis KRAS pMHC screen.

## Structural observation

On PDF p. 3, the TCR-bound G12D complex places peptide Asp6 within the HLA groove, with a salt bridge to HLA Arg114. The passage states that it

> was buried in the HLA groove and formed a salt bridge with R114

## What I use from it

The orientation of Asp6 motivates mutation-specific recognition as a problem involving the surrounding peptide–MHC surface in [[046-atlas-kras-screen#Target rationale and competition context]]. Source [Poole et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32811-1).

## Disagreements / limitations

This burial statement concerns the TCR-bound HLA-A*11:01 decamer complex. It does not establish the same side-chain orientation for other peptide lengths, HLA alleles or unbound conformations.
