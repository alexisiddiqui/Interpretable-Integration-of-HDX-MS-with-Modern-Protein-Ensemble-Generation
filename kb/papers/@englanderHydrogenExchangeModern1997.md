# Hydrogen Exchange: The Modern Legacy of Linderstr\o m-Lang. (1997)

## Main point

A review of protein hydrogen exchange, from the Linderstrøm–Lang opening–closing model to native-state HX, which uses exchange to detect partially unfolded forms and cooperative folding units.
