# Large Shifts in pKa Values of Lysine Residues Buried inside a Protein (2011)

## Main point

Lys residues engineered into 25 internal positions of staphylococcal nuclease mostly titrate with strongly depressed pKa values, some more than 5 units below the value in water, which the authors attribute to burial in a partly dehydrated, less polar environment.

## Claims
