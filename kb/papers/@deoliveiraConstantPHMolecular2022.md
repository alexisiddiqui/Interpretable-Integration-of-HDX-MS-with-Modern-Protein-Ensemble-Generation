# Constant pH Molecular Dynamics Simulations: Current Status and Recent Applications (2022)

## Main point

A short review of constant-pH molecular dynamics, which samples protonation states together with conformations at a set pH, in contrast to conventional MD with fixed protonation states. It covers the discrete and continuous families of methods and recent applications to proton-coupled conformational change.

## Claims

- Conventional MD cannot directly model proton-coupled conformational change, because protonation states are assigned in advance and held fixed. ^c1
