# Protonation and pK Changes in Protein–Ligand Binding (2013)

## Main point

Protein–ligand binding often shifts the pK values and protonation states of ionizable groups, which makes binding pH dependent. The changes are common but not universal; their estimated prevalence varies between complexes and between methods.
