# On the pH Dependence of Protein Stability (1993)

## Main point

The pH dependence of the unfolding free energy follows from the average charges of all ionizable groups in the folded and unfolded states, through Tanford's linkage relation between stability and the charge difference between states. Absolute contributions additionally need intrinsic pKa values, computed here by continuum electrostatics for hen and T4 lysozyme.
