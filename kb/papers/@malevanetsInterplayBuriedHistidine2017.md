# Interplay of Buried Histidine Protonation and Protein Stability in Prion Misfolding (2017)

## Main point

NMR titration and amide exchange at pH 5 and pH 7 on hamster and rabbit PrP(90–231) show that a single buried, conserved histidine (H187 hamster, H186 rabbit) has a pKa near 5, and that its protonation destabilises both proteins, more strongly in the aggregation-prone hamster protein.

## What I use from it

A measured, strongly down-shifted pKa for the buried His corresponding to MoPrP H186, and the link from its protonation to destabilisation, for the MoPrP pH 4 against pH 7 argument in §2.2.

## Disagreements / limitations

Hamster and rabbit PrP, not mouse; the residue is H187 in hamster and H186 in rabbit (and mouse) numbering.
