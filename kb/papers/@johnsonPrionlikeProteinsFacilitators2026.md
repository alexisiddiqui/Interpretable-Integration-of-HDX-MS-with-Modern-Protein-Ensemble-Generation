# Prion-like Proteins as Facilitators of Molecular Memory in Plants (2026)

## Main point

Opinion piece (Trends in Biochemical Sciences). Plants must integrate past and present environmental cues. The authors propose that prion-like proteins, through switch-like changes in conformation or assembly state, could store this information as persistent molecular states, and that post-translational modifications such as phosphorylation could tune their responsiveness.
