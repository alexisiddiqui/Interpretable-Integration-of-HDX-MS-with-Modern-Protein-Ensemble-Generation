# Ruggedness in the Free Energy Landscape Dictates Misfolding of the Prion Protein (2019)

## Main point

Sub-millisecond folding and unfolding kinetics of the mouse prion protein show a single shared pathway at pH 7 but distinct folding and unfolding pathways at pH 4, with unfolding at pH 4 routed through an aggregation-prone intermediate. The landscape becomes more rugged on moving to aggregation-prone conditions.

## Claims

- For the mouse prion protein, folding and unfolding use the same single pathway at pH 7, whereas at pH 4 folding follows a pathway distinct from unfolding, and the kinetics at pH 4 also depend on the initial conditions. ^c1

## What I use from it

Kinetic evidence that MoPrP behaves differently at pH 4 and pH 7, with an aggregation-prone intermediate on the pH 4 unfolding route, for the MoPrP framing in §2.2.

## Disagreements / limitations

The evidence is ensemble kinetics; the intermediate's structure is taken from earlier work, not resolved here.
