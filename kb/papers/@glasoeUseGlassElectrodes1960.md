# Use of Glass Electrodes to Measure Acidities in Deuterium Oxide (1960)

## Main point

A glass electrode standardised with H2O buffers reads 0.40 unit lower in D2O than in H2O at comparable acidity, whichever electrode is used, so pD is obtained as the meter reading plus 0.40.

## Claims

- The pD of a D2O solution is obtained from the reading of a glass electrode as pD = pH meter reading + 0.40 (Eq. 1). ^c1
