# Isotope Effects in Peptide Group Hydrogen Exchange (1993)

## Main point

Kinetic isotope effects on peptide-group hydrogen exchange are small, but acid-catalysed exchange is twice as fast in D2O, and base-catalysed rates in D2O match those in H2O only once the glass-electrode artefact and the different solvent ionisation constant are taken into account. The paper gives poly-DL-alanine reference rate constants for each isotope combination, for use with Bai et al. (1993).
