# A Molecular Mechanism for Environmental Context in Protein-Based Epigenetic Memory (2026)

## Main point

SSRN preprint, not peer reviewed. A folded RNA binding domain propagates the conserved non-amyloid prion [SMAUG+] by agglomeration. Cryo-EM and free-energy simulations show that structural polymorphism arises from distinct isoenergetic binding modes, and the RNAs bound select the polymorph. The authors present the result as a mechanism for contextualisation in cellular memory and adaptation.

## What I use from it

- The epigenetic role of prions (^c1), cited for the statement that prion conformational switching underlies epigenetic inheritance, and the environmental dependence of the state (^c2), which supports reading a conformational landscape as a record of conditions ([[prion protein]]).

## Disagreements / limitations

- A preprint, and the system is a yeast non-amyloid prion built on an RNA binding domain. It supports the principle that conformational states carry environmental history, not the specifics of the mammalian prion protein.
