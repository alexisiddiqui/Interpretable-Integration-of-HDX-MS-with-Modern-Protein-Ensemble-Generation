# Unraveling the Molecular Mechanism of pH-Induced Misfolding and Oligomerization of the Prion Protein (2016)

## Main point

In the mouse prion protein (moPrP), protonation of H186 or of D201 (in the R155–D201 salt bridge) destabilises the protein and accelerates misfolding and oligomerisation. HDX-MS shows that either protonation produces the same change, increased dynamics in helix 1 and the α1–β2 loop.

## What I use from it

The residue-level account of why MoPrP differs between pH 4 and pH 7 (H186 and D201 protonation) and the HDX-MS regions it affects, for §2.2 and the MoPrP case in ch. 5.

## Disagreements / limitations

Protonated states are mimicked by mutation (H186R, D201N) and neutralised by H186F; the mutations stand in for protonation, which is not measured site-specifically.
