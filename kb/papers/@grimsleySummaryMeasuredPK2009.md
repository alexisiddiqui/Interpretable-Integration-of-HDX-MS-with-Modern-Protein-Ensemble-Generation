# A Summary of the Measured pK Values of the Ionizable Groups in Folded Proteins (2009)

## Main point

A compilation of 541 measured pK values from 78 folded proteins gives average side-chain pK values close to the alanine-pentapeptide values for Glu and His and about 0.4 lower for Asp. Individual values spread over several pH units, and the extremes are traced to burial, hydrogen bonding and nearby charges.

## What I use from it

The average in-protein pKa values for Asp, Glu and His against the model values, the observed spread, and the three determinants of pKa shifts (charge–charge, hydrogen bonding, desolvation) for the `pKa shift` concept.

## Disagreements / limitations

The values were measured under various temperatures and ionic strengths, mostly by NMR, so the averages mix conditions.
