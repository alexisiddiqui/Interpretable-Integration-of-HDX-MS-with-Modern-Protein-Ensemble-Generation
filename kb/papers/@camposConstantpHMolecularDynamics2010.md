# Constant-pH Molecular Dynamics Simulations Reveal a β-Rich Form of the Human Prion Protein (2010)

## Main point

Constant-pH MD of human PrP(90–231) between pH 2 and 7 shows helix content falling and β content rising towards acidic pH, with the main rearrangements in the C-terminal core. Protonation of the low-pKa His187 weakens the contact between two subdomains, and one pH 2 simulation formed a stable β-rich structure.

## What I use from it

A simulation precedent for protonation-driven rearrangement of PrP at low pH, centred on the His187 region (mouse H186), alongside the experimental evidence from [[@singhUnravelingMolecularMechanism2016]].

## Disagreements / limitations

Human PrP and human numbering (His187 corresponds to mouse H186). The β-rich form appeared in one pH 2 simulation, below the pH 4 condition used for MoPrP, and the authors note there were no experimental pKa values to compare with.
