# A Scrapie-like Unfolding Intermediate of the Prion Protein Domain PrP(121–231) Induced by Acidic pH (1998)

## Main point

Urea unfolding of murine PrP(121–231) followed by far-UV CD is an apparent two-state transition at pH 6.0 and 7.0, but at pH 4.0 and 4.5 it passes through an equilibrium intermediate with a β-sheet-like spectrum. The intermediate forms with an apparent pKa of 4.5, which the authors take as evidence that acidic side chains are protonated on its formation.

## What I use from it

A case where a modest drop in pH (7.0 to 4.0) changes which conformational states a domain populates, through protonation of acidic residues; cited where pH is introduced as a variable that reshapes the ensemble and not only the intrinsic exchange rate.

## Disagreements / limitations

The "β-sheet" assignment rests on far-UV CD alone, with spectra in urea limited to above 210 nm.
