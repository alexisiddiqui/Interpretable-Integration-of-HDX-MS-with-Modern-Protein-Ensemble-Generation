## Argument

Zhang and Gong propose frontier expansion sampling as a new enhanced sampling method that improves the sampling efficiency of molecular simulations.

## Caveats

Zhang and Gong call the method FEXS. They also note that artificially boosting sampling around the frontier structures may disrupt the Boltzmann distribution of sampled structures (p2), which is relevant to the later uncited statement that FES preserves the equilibrium distribution.
