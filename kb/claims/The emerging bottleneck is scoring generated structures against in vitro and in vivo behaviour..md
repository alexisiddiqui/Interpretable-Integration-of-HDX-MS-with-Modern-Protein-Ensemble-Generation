## Argument

Hummer and Köfinger say that ensemble refinement against experimental data is becoming increasingly important as dynamic and partially disordered biomolecular structures come into focus.

## Caveats

The paper does not say that scoring structures against experiment is the emerging bottleneck; it states the growing importance of refining ensembles against experimental data, which is related but weaker.
