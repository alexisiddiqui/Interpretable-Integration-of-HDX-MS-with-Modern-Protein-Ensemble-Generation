## Argument

Abriata and Dal Peraro say a limitation of most MD studies is the limited timescale of sampling relative to the timescale of relevant molecular motions, and report that several forcefields could not accurately describe the dynamics of disordered proteins. Hollingsworth et al. say simulations are very computationally demanding, that force fields are inherently approximate, and that important processes often take place on timescales longer than classical all-atom MD can reach.

## Caveats

Abriata and Dal Peraro do not themselves state that MD is computationally expensive; that part rests on Hollingsworth (p2).
