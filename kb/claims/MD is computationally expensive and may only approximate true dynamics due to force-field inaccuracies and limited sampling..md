## Argument

Abriata and Dal Peraro say a limitation of most MD studies is the limited timescale of sampling relative to the timescale of relevant molecular motions, and report that several forcefields could not accurately describe the dynamics of disordered proteins.

## Caveats

Abriata and Dal Peraro do not state that MD is computationally expensive. @hollingsworthMolecularDynamicsSimulation2018 has no PDF so it could not be checked.
