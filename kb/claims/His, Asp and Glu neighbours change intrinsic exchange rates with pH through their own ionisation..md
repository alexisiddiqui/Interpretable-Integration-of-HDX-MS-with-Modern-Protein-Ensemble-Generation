## Argument

Bai et al. needed separate rate constants for the protonated and unprotonated forms of His, Asp and Glu neighbours, with side-chain pKa values in D₂O at 5 °C of 7.02, 4.53 and 4.08 ([[@baiPrimaryStructureEffects1993#^c5]]). The neighbour factor of these residues is therefore a function of pD, and at pD 4 it differs from its value at pD 7 ([[intrinsic exchange rate]]).

## Caveats

The pKa values are for dipeptides; in a protein the neighbour's own [[pKa shift]] moves the crossover. Whether the intrinsic-rate code used for the jaxENT MoPrP fits at pD 4 applies the ionisation-dependent factors has not been checked.
