## Argument

The ATLAS paper says MD simulations have given useful information on conformational behaviour for decades, that dynamics analysis is held back by a lack of reliable data, and that its own dataset has 1390 protein chains. The MISATO paper provides molecular dynamics simulations of about 20,000 experimental protein-ligand complexes. Together they span the thousands to tens of thousands of entries in the sentence.
