## Argument

The ATLAS paper says MD simulations have given useful information on conformational behaviour for decades, that dynamics analysis is held back by a lack of reliable data, and that its own dataset has 1390 protein chains.

## Caveats

ATLAS supports the 'thousands' end and the decades-old tools. The 'tens of thousands' end rests on the MISATO paper, which has no PDF.
