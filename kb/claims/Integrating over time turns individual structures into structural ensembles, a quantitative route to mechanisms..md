## Argument

Hummer and Köfinger treat experimental observables as averages over an ensemble of structures, so a single structure is not the right comparison object. This supports the idea of working with [[structural ensemble|structural ensembles]], but not that they are obtained by integrating over time.

## Caveats

The cited paper is about Bayesian refinement of ensembles against experimental data. It does not state that ensembles are built by integrating over time, nor that this is a route from static structures to biological mechanisms.
