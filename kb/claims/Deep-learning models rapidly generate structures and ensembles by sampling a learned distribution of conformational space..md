## Argument

Zheng et al. describe generative deep learning models that learn a probability distribution of the training data and generate new samples at low computational cost. Noé et al. train [[Boltzmann generators]] to produce unbiased equilibrium samples of proteins in one shot instead of by many small simulation steps.
