## Argument

James says HDX-MS can be applied to nearly any protein system, including many too large or heterogeneous for other structural tools, and Marciano describes it as complementing NMR and crystallography and as able to interrogate large proteins and complexes.

## Caveats

Neither paper states that HDX-MS has no size limitation or that it is uniquely able to resolve dynamics under diverse conditions. James elsewhere (p16) notes a size limit set by the platform's ability to resolve and detect all peptides. The passages support the orthogonality and the breadth of applicability, not the absolute wording.
