## Argument

Hummer and Köfinger state that dynamic and partially disordered biomolecular systems play central roles in biology and in disease.

## Caveats

The paper does not say that the mechanism of misfunction stays unclear without measurements of dynamics; it supports only the role of dynamic, disordered systems in disease.
