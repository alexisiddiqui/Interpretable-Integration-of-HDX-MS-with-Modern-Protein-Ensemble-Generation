## Argument

Each checked paper pairs [[HDX-MS]] data with molecular dynamics simulations to generate or evaluate candidate structural ensembles.

Also stated in: milestones/2025-11-confirmation-of-status/introduction/connect_simulation_experiment.tex#L89 ("To overcome this, we can leverage simulations to generate putative structural ensembles").

## Caveats

The papers are individual examples of using simulations alongside HDX-MS; none states that this practice is increasing. Puchades et al., cited with these papers in the manuscript, is an experimental epitope-mapping study with no simulations, so it is not cited here.

For the confirmation-report sentence (L89), support is by example: Hummer and Koefinger's cited passage (^c5) is about refining ensembles with coupled replica molecular dynamics simulations, and Anderson et al.'s (^c2, ^c3) applies MD to an immune complex and mentions ensemble pruning or restraint with HDX-MS data. Neither states that simulations are used to generate candidate ensembles as such.
