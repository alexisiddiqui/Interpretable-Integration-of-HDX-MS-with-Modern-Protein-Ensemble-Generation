## Argument

Each checked paper pairs [[HDX-MS]] data with molecular dynamics simulations to generate or evaluate candidate structural ensembles.

## Caveats

The papers are individual examples of using simulations alongside HDX-MS; none states that this practice is increasing. Puchades et al. is an experimental epitope-mapping study and mentions no simulations.
