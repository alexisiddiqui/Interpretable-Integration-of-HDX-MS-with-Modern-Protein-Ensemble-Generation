## Argument

Energy landscape theory holds that natural proteins navigate a rough funnel ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c31]]), which is possible for sequences that follow the principle of minimal frustration. Galpern et al. give a quantitative check: the folding selection temperature of 64 domains lies below the folding temperature, as minimal frustration requires ([[@galpernProbingDarkEnergy2026#^c4]]). Minimal frustration explains why a sequence folds; §1.1 group C turns to what it leaves unresolved.

## Caveats

Parra et al. is a review; the primary references are Bryngelson and Wolynes (1987) and Onuchic et al. (1997), neither yet in the KB. The selection temperature uses ESM-2 scores as an evolutionary energy, which is an approximation.
