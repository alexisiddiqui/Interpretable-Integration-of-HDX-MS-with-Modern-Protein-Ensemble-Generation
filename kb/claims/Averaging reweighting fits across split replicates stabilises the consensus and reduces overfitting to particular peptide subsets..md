## Argument

The revised ValDX results state that averaging results across replicate fits improves stability and reduces the risk of overfitting to any specific subset of observed peptides (`7_results_omc.tex` L60). The consensus is the arithmetic mean of weights and coefficients across split replicates (`6_methods_omc.tex` L659, L672) ([[model averaging]], [[Peptide splitting and consensus (ValDX)]]).

## Caveats

Not in posted v1, and no result in the manuscript compares consensus with single fits. The principle is carried by the general source; this claim needs a figure to stand on its own.
