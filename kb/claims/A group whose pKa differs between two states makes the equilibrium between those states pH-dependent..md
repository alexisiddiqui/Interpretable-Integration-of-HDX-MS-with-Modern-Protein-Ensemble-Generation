## Argument

Yang and Honig compute the pH dependence of folding free energy from the pKa values of ionisable groups in the folded and unfolded states ([[@yangPHDependenceProtein1993#^c1]]). The same linkage holds for binding: a group whose pKa changes on binding makes the binding affinity depend on pH ([[@onufrievProtonationPKChanges2013#^c1]]). The general form is thermodynamic linkage between proton binding and the conformational or binding equilibrium ([[pKa]]).

## Caveats

Both sources are computational frameworks validated against experiment for particular proteins. For MoPrP, the states in question are native, partially unfolded and misfolded forms, and the relevant groups are those whose pKa differs between them.
