## Argument

## Caveats

The source says NMR-derived ensembles and does not give the '~20 frames' per ensemble, the 'as of 2025' date, or the phrase 'capture limited dynamics'. The two sentences of the source paragraph share one cite and are combined here; the sentence on sequence databases has no cite and is not covered.
