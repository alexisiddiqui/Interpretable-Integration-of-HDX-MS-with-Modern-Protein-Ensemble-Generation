## Argument

Abrudan et al. state that only roughly 12,000 NMR-derived protein ensembles exist, covering just 21% of CATH superfamilies, and describe multi-conformational data as far more scarce than individual PDB chains.

## Caveats

The source says NMR-derived ensembles. The '~20 frames' per ensemble and 'as of 2025' details in the sentence are the author's additions and are not stated. The two sentences of the source paragraph share one cite and are combined here; the sentence on sequence databases has no cite and is not covered.

Abrudan et al. hedge the scarcity ("at a first glance") and refer to NMR-derived ensembles only; the sentence states it without either qualification.
