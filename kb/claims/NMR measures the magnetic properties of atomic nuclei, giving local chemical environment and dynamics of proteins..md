## Argument

Kleckner and Foster state that [[NMR]] exploits the magnetic properties of certain nuclei and is exquisitely sensitive to local structure and dynamics in many time windows. Kay describes NMR as one of the most powerful tools for studying protein structure and dynamics, which corroborates the dynamics part.

## Caveats

Kay's passage supports only the structure-and-dynamics part of the sentence; it does not mention magnetic properties of nuclei or the local chemical environment, which rest on Kleckner and Foster.
