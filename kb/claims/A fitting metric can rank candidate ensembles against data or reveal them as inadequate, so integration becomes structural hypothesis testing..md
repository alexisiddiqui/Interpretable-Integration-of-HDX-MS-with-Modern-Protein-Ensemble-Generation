## Argument

Bradshaw et al. describe a quantitative metric with which candidate ensembles can be ranked or revealed to be inadequate. ValDX frames HDX-MS ensemble integration as a quantitative structural hypothesis-testing problem.

## Caveats

Ranking is relative to the candidates offered; the best of a poor set can still be wrong ([[sampling coverage]]).
