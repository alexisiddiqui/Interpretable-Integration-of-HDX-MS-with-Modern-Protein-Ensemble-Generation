## Argument

Casey and Sleator state that prions, potentially among the oldest molecules on Earth, have origins dating back to the emergence of life ([[@caseyPrionsStructureFunction2024#^c3]]). They support this by the resilience of prions to radiation, heat, crosslinking and enzymatic digestion, which suggests their existence in the harsh environments of early Earth, and by their autocatalytic self-replication and assembly. Their conformational diversity lets them adapt to varied environments. See [[prion protein]].

## Caveats

The passage carries its own qualifier ("potentially"), which the claim keeps. The prion-first hypothesis, that prion-like mechanisms acted in early biochemical evolution before DNA and protein-based life, is a separate hypothesis in the same passage and is not part of this claim.
