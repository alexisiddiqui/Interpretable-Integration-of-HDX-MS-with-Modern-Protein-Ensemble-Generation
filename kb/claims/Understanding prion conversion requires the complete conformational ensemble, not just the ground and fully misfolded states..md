## Argument

Hummer and Koefinger say experimental observables report on averages over the whole ensemble, and Koefinger et al. describe ensemble refinement as producing structural ensembles of dynamic biomolecules by combining data with simulations.

## Caveats

Neither paper discusses prion conversion or intermediate states between the ground and misfolded states.
