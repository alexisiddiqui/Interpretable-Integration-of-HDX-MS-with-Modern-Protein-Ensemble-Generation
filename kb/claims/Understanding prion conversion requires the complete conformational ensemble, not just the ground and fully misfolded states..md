## Argument

Biancaniello et al. say that characterising the structure of the intermediate is critical to elucidating how the T183A prion protein variant converts to the scrapie form, and that X-ray crystallography, NMR and cryo-EM cannot capture dynamic intermediate species.

## Caveats

The passages concern the T183A variant, and neither states that the complete ensemble is required. The step from the intermediate to the whole ensemble is the thesis's own framing. Hummer and Koefinger and Koefinger et al. were cited here before but do not discuss prion conversion.
