## Argument

Mukherjee et al. say [[protein misfolding and aggregation|amyloid nucleation]] is poorly understood because instruments have a spatial and temporal resolution limit for detecting transient events, which supports the idea that aggregation-prone states can escape standard methods.

## Caveats

No checked passage states the contrast in the sentence: that cryo-EM shows helical fibrils while the solution state is disordered. Mukherjee mentions a cryo-EM fibril structure only in a Figure 1 inset, and says disordered alpha-synuclein monomers convert to helix-rich oligomers (p4), which is not the fibril-versus-solution contrast. The cryo-EM structure itself is cited to Ahmed et al. in the preceding sentence.
