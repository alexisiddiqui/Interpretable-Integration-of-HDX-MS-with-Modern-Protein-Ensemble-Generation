## Argument

Borg et al. show that [[knowledge-based potential]]s are in general not free energies or potentials of mean force in the statistical-mechanics sense, that their 'energy' differs from the physical energy of the state, and that there is no a priori reason to read a particular one as a free energy. Frellsen et al. explain them as statistical constructs.

## Caveats

The introduction applies this to generator scores. That transfer is our argument; BMSB discusses database-derived potentials.
