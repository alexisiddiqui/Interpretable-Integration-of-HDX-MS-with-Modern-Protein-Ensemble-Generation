## Argument

When a knowledge-based potential is used with a prior or sampler, its reference state is uniquely determined by that sampler, and the reference free energy is the free energy of the prior. This explicit construction is the [[reference ratio method]].

## Caveats

The reference ratio imposes a whole coarse-grained marginal; MaxEnt reweighting imposes a few averages. The analogy is conceptual (reference ratio method, step 6).
