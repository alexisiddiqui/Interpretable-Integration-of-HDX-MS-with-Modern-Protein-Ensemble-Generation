## Argument

Lisi and Loria describe the distinct [[X-ray crystallography|crystal structures]] of PKA as time-averaged conformations of the structural ensemble governing its function ([[@lisiAllosteryEnzymeCatalysis2017#^c5]]). The structures identify conformations but do not describe the motions between them.

## Caveats

The example concerns PKA crystal structures. It does not establish the temporal resolution of other experimental techniques.
