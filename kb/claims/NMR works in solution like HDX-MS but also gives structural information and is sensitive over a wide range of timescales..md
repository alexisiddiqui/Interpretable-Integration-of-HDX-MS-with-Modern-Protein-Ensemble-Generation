## Argument

Sapienza and Lee say [[NMR]] is well suited to characterising protein dynamics in solution at atomic resolution and is sensitive to essentially the whole ps to days range of protein motions. Kleckner and Foster describe its sensitivity to local structure and dynamics in many time windows.

## Caveats

The contrast 'unlike HDX-MS' is the author's comparison; the checked papers describe NMR alone.
