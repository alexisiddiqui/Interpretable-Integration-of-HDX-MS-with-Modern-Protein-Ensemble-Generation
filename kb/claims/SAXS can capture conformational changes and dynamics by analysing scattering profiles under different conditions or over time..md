## Argument

Ahmed et al. say NMR diffusion and [[SAXS]] experiments are especially useful to quantify the compaction of an intrinsically disordered protein.

## Caveats

The passage concerns measuring compaction of an IDP ensemble; it does not describe following conformational changes by analysing profiles under different conditions or over time.
