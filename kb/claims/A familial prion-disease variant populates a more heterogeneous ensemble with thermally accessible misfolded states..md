## Argument

The T183A [[prion protein]] variant, associated with familial Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease ([[@biancanielloStructureDynamicsMisfolding2025#^c5]]), gave restrained ensembles with greater conformational heterogeneity than wild type ([[@biancanielloStructureDynamicsMisfolding2025#^c4]]). Its partially misfolded states are thermally accessible, which explains the absent lag phase in its aggregation ([[@biancanielloStructureDynamicsMisfolding2025#^c10]]). A single mutation shifts weight towards misfolding-competent states: a [[population shift]] with a kinetic consequence.

## Caveats

The ensembles come from simulations restrained by experimental data, so they inherit the model and restraints. The protein is the system later used in jaxENT, which makes the example convenient but not independent.
