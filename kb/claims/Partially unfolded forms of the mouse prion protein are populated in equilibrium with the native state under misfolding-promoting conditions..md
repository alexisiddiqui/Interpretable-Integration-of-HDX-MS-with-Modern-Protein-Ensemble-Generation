## Argument

At pH 4, where the mouse prion protein is primed to misfold, different segments exchange through subglobal unfolding events of different extent ([[@moulickPartiallyUnfoldedForms2015#^c1]]). The data suggest that the native state is in equilibrium with at least two partially unfolded forms ([[@moulickPartiallyUnfoldedForms2015#^c2]]). Their populations relative to the native state follow from the free energy of opening, $\pi_B/\pi_A = \exp(-\Delta G_{AB}/k_BT)$, so a measured $\Delta G_\text{op}$ places each form on a population scale (left panel of `fig:intro-prion-populations`). See [[prion protein]] and [[high-energy intermediate]].

## Caveats

The paper's own wording is "suggest". The populations are extrapolated through a thermodynamic model from denaturant titrations and are not observed directly at zero denaturant (see the limitations in the paper note).
