## Argument

Conventional peptide-level [[HDX-MS]] reports uptake averaged over the exchangeable amides within each fragment ([[@leeInterpretingHydrogenDeuteriumExchange2021#^c1]]). Digestion produces overlapping peptides, typically of 5 to 30 residues ([[@vinciauskaiteFundamentalsHDXMS2023#^c5]]). The measured fragment uptake therefore does not directly assign uptake to individual residues ([[deuterium uptake]]).

### Historical title

### Historical argument

> Lee et al. describe HDX-MS exchange rates as averaged over oligopeptide fragments, and Mohammadiarani et al. state that HDX-MS results only provide fragment-level resolution. Vinciauskaite et al. describe the digestion as giving numerous overlapping peptides of 5 to 30 residues.
