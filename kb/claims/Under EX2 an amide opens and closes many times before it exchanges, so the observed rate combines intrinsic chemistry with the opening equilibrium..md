## Argument

James et al. state that when reclosing is much faster than chemical exchange, an amide undergoes many opening motions before it is deuterated. Vinciauskaite et al. describe each opening event as an opportunity for exchange. Skinner et al. give the EX2 rate as the chemical rate times the fraction of time open.

## Caveats

EX2 is an assumption about each amide; EX1 and mixed kinetics occur at high pH or for slow cooperative unfolding ([[Linderstrøm–Lang model]]).
