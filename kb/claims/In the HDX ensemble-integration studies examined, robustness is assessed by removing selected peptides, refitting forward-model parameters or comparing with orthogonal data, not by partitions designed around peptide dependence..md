## Argument

The XylE study tested sensitivity to re-optimised Best–Vendruscolo coefficients and concluded the interpretation was relatively robust to small forward-model uncertainties ([[@jiaIntegratingHydrogenDeuterium2023#^c4]], [[@jiaIntegratingHydrogenDeuterium2023#^c5]]). HDXer's authors propose splitting time points ([[@bradshawInterpretationHDXData2020#^c20]]). Each check answers a question; none withholds peptides around their dependence ([[data splitting]]).

## Caveats

A survey statement, scoped to the studies in the vault. Other integrative fields do withhold restraints ([[model selection]], Burnley et al.).
