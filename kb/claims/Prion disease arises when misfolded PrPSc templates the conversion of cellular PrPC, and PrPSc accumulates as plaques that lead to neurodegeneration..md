## Argument

The abnormal PrPSc molecule acts as a template that induces the normally folded PrPC to misfold ([[@caseyPrionsStructureFunction2024#^c1]]). The newly converted conformations propagate and accumulate, leading to plaque formation and cell death, primarily in neurons ([[@caseyPrionsStructureFunction2024#^c2]]). This is panel B of `fig:intro-prion-populations`. See [[prion protein]] and [[protein misfolding and aggregation]].

## Caveats

Casey and Sleator review the mechanism and cite the primary studies for it.
