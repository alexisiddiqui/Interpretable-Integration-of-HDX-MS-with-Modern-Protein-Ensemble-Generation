## Argument

Narang et al. say the protein amount needed for [[HDX-MS]] is a fraction of that needed by alternative biophysical techniques, and that its great advantage is versatility, since virtually any system can be explored.

## Caveats

Sensitivity is not stated as a benefit in the checked passages, and 'flexibility' is matched to Narang's 'versatility', which is a different property from flexibility.
