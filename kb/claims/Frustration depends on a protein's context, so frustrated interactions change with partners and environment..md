## Argument

Frustration depends on the protein's context within a biological system ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c29]]). IκBα is more frustrated as an isolated monomer than in its complex with NFκB, because the partner's compensating interactions are missing ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c28]]). A protein studied on its own therefore shows frustration that its partners and environment would resolve or change. See [[local frustration]] and [[Conformational populations depend on molecular context, so they are defined only for a stated environment.]]

## Caveats

The evidence is one complex (IκBα with NFκB) and the review's general statement. The generalisation to "environment" is the thesis's reading.
