## Argument

Abramson et al. report that AlphaFold 3 predicts protein-ligand interactions much more accurately than state-of-the-art docking tools, and say substantial challenges remain in achieving highly accurate predictions across all interaction types.

## Caveats

The paper states remaining accuracy challenges, not a question about generalisability to unseen systems. It also claims AlphaFold 3 generalises well.
