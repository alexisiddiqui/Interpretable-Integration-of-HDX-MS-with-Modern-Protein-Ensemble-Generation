## Argument

Hummer and Köfinger describe ensemble refinement using coupled replica [[molecular dynamics]] simulations, and Anderson et al. use MD simulations to understand conformational dynamics, noting that groups have pruned ensembles or restrained simulations using HDX-MS data.

## Caveats

Neither paper states that simulations are used to generate candidate ensembles as such. Hummer and Köfinger's passage is about refining ensembles with replica simulations, and Anderson et al. apply MD to a specific immune complex, so the support is by example.
