## Argument

A harmonic restraint on replica-averaged observables gives the same ensemble as the maximum-entropy approach as the number of replicas grows ([[restrained simulation]], [[maximum entropy principle]]).

## Caveats

Only in the limit; the force constant must grow faster than the number of replicas to enforce the average exactly (Cesari ^c19). With noisy data the Bayesian treatment scales it linearly instead (Hummer ^c9).
