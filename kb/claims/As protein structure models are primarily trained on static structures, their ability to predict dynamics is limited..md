## Argument

Casadevall et al. say AF2 was designed to predict single static structures and that a single static folded structure is not sufficient for understanding function. Wilson et al. say the dynamics of protein folding remains a formidable problem.

## Caveats

None of the checked passages says the models are trained primarily on static structures, so the causal link from training data to limited dynamics is the author's.
