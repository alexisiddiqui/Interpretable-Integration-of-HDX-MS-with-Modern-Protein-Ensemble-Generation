## Argument

Lisi and Loria note that conformational ensembles are highly tunable in many systems, which is advantageous for biomedical applications. Mukherjee et al. say understanding the nucleation and early events of amyloid aggregation is particularly important for designing therapeutics against aggregation. These are the closest checked statements to dynamics informing therapeutics.

## Caveats

Neither paper says that understanding dynamics is 'the key' to new therapies, nor that evidence is 'mounting'. Lisi and Loria is a short review of allostery in three enzymes, and Mukherjee's statement concerns aggregation mechanisms in general, not dynamics specifically.
