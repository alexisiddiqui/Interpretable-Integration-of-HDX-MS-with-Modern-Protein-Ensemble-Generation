## Argument

Lisi and Loria note that conformational ensembles are highly tunable in many systems, which is advantageous for biomedical applications; this is the closest checked statement to dynamics informing therapeutics.

## Caveats

The paper does not say that understanding dynamics is 'the key' to new therapies, nor that evidence is 'mounting'; it is a short review of allostery in three enzymes.
