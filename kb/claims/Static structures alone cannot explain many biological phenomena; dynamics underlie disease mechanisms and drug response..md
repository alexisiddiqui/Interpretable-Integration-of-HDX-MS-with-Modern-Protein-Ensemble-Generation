## Argument

Hummer and Koefinger say dynamic and partially disordered systems play central roles in biology and disease, Lee et al. note that single structures underuse the information in HDX measurements, and Lisi and Loria say allostery has an expanding role in disease and drug discovery.

## Caveats

No checked passage says that static structures cannot explain many biological phenomena in general, and none links dynamics to drug response; Lee's point is specific to interpreting HDX data and Lisi's to allostery.
