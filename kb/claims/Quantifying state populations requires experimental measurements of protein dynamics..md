## Argument

Lee et al. describe HDXer reweighting the conformational populations of a candidate ensemble to conform to experimental exchange data, and state that next-generation HDX studies need objective structural ensembles derived directly from the measurements.

## Caveats

The passages concern HDX data specifically and show data being used to set populations; they do not state in general that experimental dynamics measurements are needed.
