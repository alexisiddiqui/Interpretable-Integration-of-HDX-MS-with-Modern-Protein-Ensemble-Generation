## Argument

Lee et al. say HDXer protection factors are translated into an overall exchange rate and fractional deuteration for any peptide segment and time point, allowing direct comparison to HDX-MS data. Bradshaw et al. calculate protection factors per residue in order to predict deuterium uptake from structural snapshots.
