## Argument

Differential HDX shows ligand-specific changes in the conformational ensemble of the β2-adrenergic receptor ([[@westLigandDependentPerturbationConformational2011#^c1]]). Frustration analysis gives the energetic reason: IκBα is more frustrated alone than bound to NFκB ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c28]]), calmodulin's Ca²⁺ sites look frustrated only when the ion is absent ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c30]]), and frustration depends on the protein's context ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c29]]). Every species that binds states unequally shifts their populations ([[dynamic disease mechanism#^eq-environment-linkage]]), so a population is defined only for a stated environment. This is one reason to measure in solution, under stated conditions.

## Caveats

The general statement is our inference from these cases and from ligand linkage. Crowding and other in-cell effects are not covered by the KB sources.
