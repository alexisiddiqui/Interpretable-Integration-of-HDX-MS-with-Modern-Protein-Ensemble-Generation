## Argument

Folding minimises conflicts, but not all of them: the folded structure stays locally frustrated ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c1]]). Across single-domain proteins, about 40% of contacts are minimally frustrated and form the core ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c2]]), and about 10% are highly frustrated and cluster in surface patches ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c3]]). Minimal frustration is a global property; it leaves local conflicts that the rest of the structure holds in place.

## Caveats

Percentages come from the Frustratometer (AWSEM) applied to single structures; the primary source is Ferreiro et al. (2007), not yet in the KB. The classes depend on decoy statistics and thresholds.
