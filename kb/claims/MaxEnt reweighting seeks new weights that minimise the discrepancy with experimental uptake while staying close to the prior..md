## Argument

Cesari et al. say the [[maximum entropy principle]] yields the posterior closest to the prior that agrees with the experimental observations, and Crehuet et al. say maximum entropy produces the minimum perturbation to the original ensemble that fits the data.

## Caveats

The cited papers state the general principle (the posterior closest to the prior that agrees with the data, or the minimum perturbation that fits the data). Neither mentions HDX uptake data or fitting predicted deuterium uptake; Crehuet et al. work with NMR chemical shifts, and Cesari et al. review general ensemble-average constraints. Lee et al. p9 (block ^c10, not cited here) describes the HDXer objective with a smallest-bias term and an error term on the uptake.
