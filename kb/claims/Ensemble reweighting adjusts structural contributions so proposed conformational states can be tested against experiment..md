## Argument

Lee, Jia (2023), Bradshaw and Kihn describe [[ensemble reweighting]] of candidate ensembles to fit HDX-MS data, Bradshaw adds a metric that ranks candidate ensembles by agreement with the data or shows them to be inadequate, and Wan et al. infer which ensemble agrees best with experiment.

## Caveats

The manuscript sentence also cites Mohammadiarani et al., Puchades et al. and Jia et al. (2020). None of them describes reweighting, so they are not cited here. Wan et al. use Bayesian inference over MSM ensembles, which is related to but not identical with maximum-entropy reweighting.
