## Argument

Lee, Jia (2023), Bradshaw and Kihn describe [[ensemble reweighting]] of candidate ensembles to fit HDX-MS data, Bradshaw adds a metric that ranks candidate ensembles by agreement with the data or shows them to be inadequate, and Wan et al. infer which ensemble agrees best with experiment.

## Caveats

Three of the eight cited papers have no supporting passage. Mohammadiarani et al. compare MD-based protection-factor models with HDX-MS data but do not reweight ensembles. Puchades et al. mentions no simulation or reweighting. Jia et al. (2020) combine HDX-MS with MD simulations to corroborate experiments but no reweighting was found. Wan et al. use Bayesian inference over MSM ensembles, which is related to but not identical with maximum-entropy reweighting.
