## Argument

Adaptive seeding selects starting structures using a representation of conformational space. The confirmation manuscript describes the use of specific collective variables or coordinates obtained by dimensionality reduction (`milestones/2025-11-confirmation-of-status/introduction/ensemble_generators.tex` L80). Zhang and Gong discuss using different coordinate systems before PCA or another dimensionality-reduction method in FEXS ([[@zhangFrontierExpansionSampling2020#^c3]]).

## Caveats

Seed selection depends on the coordinate representation and the dimensionality-reduction method ([[enhanced sampling]]).
