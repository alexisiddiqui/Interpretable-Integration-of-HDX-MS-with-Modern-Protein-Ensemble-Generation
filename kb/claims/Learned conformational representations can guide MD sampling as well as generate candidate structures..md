## Argument

Zheng et al. describe learned low-dimensional representations of protein conformational space that can be used to drive additional MD sampling or generate new conformations ([[@zhengMachineLearningGeneration2023#^c4]]).

## Caveats

The two uses of the learned representation are distinct. Guiding MD sampling does not by itself establish the accuracy of the resulting ensemble.
