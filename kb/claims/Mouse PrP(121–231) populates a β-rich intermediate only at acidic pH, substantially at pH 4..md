## Argument

Hornemann and Glockshuber report a scrapie-like, β-sheet-rich unfolding intermediate of the mouse PrP domain 121–231 that appears only at acidic pH and is substantially populated at pH 4.0 ([[@hornemannScrapielikeUnfoldingIntermediate1998]], from the abstract).

## Caveats

No PDF yet, so no quote block; the claim stays draft until `kb/_pdf/@hornemannScrapielikeUnfoldingIntermediate1998.pdf` exists and a block is added. The construct is a truncated domain (121–231), not the full-length MoPrP of the HDX-MS data.
