## Argument

HDX data reflect an ensemble of conformations and might not be explained by a single structural state ([[@bradshawInterpretationHDXData2020#^c6]]). Combining enhanced-sampling MD with HDXer revealed an apo-PhuS landscape that differs from the crystal structure ([[@kihnModelingNativeEnsemble2021#^c1]]), and BICePs has used HDX protection factors to estimate the populations of conformational states ([[@wanReconcilingSimulatedEnsembles2020#^c2]]). [[HDX-MS]] is therefore a measurement that can constrain populations, which §1.2 introduces.

## Caveats

Both examples rely on a forward model and a candidate ensemble; what the inferred populations mean is the subject of §1.4–1.5.
