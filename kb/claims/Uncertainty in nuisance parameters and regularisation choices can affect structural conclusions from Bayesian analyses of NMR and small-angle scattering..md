## Argument

Habeck estimates the data weight simultaneously with the structure, and the estimated error balances the sources of information. Hansen's hyperparameter sets the strength of the prior relative to the data, and averaging over hyperparameters gives the error estimate for the result. See [[nuisance parameter]] and [[regularisation]].

## Caveats

A quote from Habeck's comparison with cross-validation (Fig. 12.10) or Hansen's error bars (Fig. 13.8) showing a changed structure would make this supported.
