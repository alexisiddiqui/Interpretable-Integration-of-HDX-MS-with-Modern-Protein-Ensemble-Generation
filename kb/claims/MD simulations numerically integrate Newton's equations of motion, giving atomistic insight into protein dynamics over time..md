## Argument

Karplus and McCammon say simulations can give the ultimate detail on individual particle motions as a function of time. Hollingsworth et al. say Newton's laws of motion are used to predict each atom's position over time, by stepping through time and repeatedly updating forces, positions and velocities, which is numerical integration.

## Caveats

Neither paper states 'equations of motion' or 'numerical integration'; Karplus and McCammon do not mention Newton's laws at all; that part, and the step-by-step integration, rest on Hollingsworth (p2), which describes it without using the words 'numerical integration'.
