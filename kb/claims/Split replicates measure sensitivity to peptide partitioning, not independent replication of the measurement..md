## Argument

Split replicates in staged protocols share the consensus weights or parameters of the preceding stage, so their variation describes sensitivity to peptide partitioning and not independent measurement-noise replication (`6_methods_omc.tex` L693). ValDX keeps this apart from the interlaboratory reproducibility of uptake (L606), and the supplement's global and local confidence are split-specific reproducibility, not calibrated probabilities (`9_Supplementary_information.tex` L89) ([[replicate uncertainty]], [[experimental uncertainty]]).
