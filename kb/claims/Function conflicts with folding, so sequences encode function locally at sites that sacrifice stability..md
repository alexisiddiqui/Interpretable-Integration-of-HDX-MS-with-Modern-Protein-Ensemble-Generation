## Argument

Galpern et al. find a trade-off in which fixing functional sites compromises folding ([[@galpernProbingDarkEnergy2026#^c8]]). Folding is encoded globally, while function can be coded locally ([[@galpernProbingDarkEnergy2026#^c9]]). Evolutionary constraint beyond folding concentrates at functional and frustrated sites ([[@galpernProbingDarkEnergy2026#^c2]]) and covers about a quarter of positions in folded globular proteins ([[@galpernProbingDarkEnergy2026#^c3]]). The sequence pays for function in stability, at specific places.

## Caveats

"About a quarter" depends on a Gaussian-mixture threshold and on the folding-energy method (25–35%). The evolutionary scores come from ESM-2, which is an approximation to a Boltzmann-like potential.
