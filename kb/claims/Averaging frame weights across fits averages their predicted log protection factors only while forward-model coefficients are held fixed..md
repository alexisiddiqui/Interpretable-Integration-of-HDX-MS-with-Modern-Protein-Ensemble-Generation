## Argument

The ensemble-average log protection factor is linear in the weights ([[Best–Vendruscolo model#^eq-bv-ensemble]]), so the mean of the weight vectors gives the mean of the profiles when the coefficients are fixed. With the coefficients also fitted the prediction is bilinear, and the mean of the products differs from the product of the means by the covariance of weights and coefficients across fits, which compensation makes non-zero ([[model averaging]], [[model misspecification]]).

## Caveats

Step ours. Peptide uptake is non-linear in the rates, so even with fixed coefficients the averaging of uptake is approximate ([[deuterium uptake]]).
