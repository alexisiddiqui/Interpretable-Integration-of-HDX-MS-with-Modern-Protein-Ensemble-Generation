## Argument

Ahmed et al. say monomeric alpha-synuclein is disordered and populates multiple conformational states.

## Caveats

The paper is a SAXS ensemble-refinement study; it does not mention cryo-EM structures of Lewy body fibrils or give a 14 kDa mass (it says 140 residues).
