## Argument

Ahmed et al. say monomeric [[alpha-synuclein]] is disordered and populates multiple conformational states. Yan et al. solved cryo-EM structures of α-synuclein filaments from dementia-with-Lewy-bodies tissue at 2.3 to 2.9 Å ([[@yanCryoEMStructuresInfectious2026#^c1]]), and Dhavale et al. found the same fold by solid-state NMR ([[@dhavaleStructureAlphasynucleinFibrils2024#^c1]]).

## Caveats

Ahmed et al. is a SAXS ensemble-refinement study; it supports only the disordered monomer and does not give a 14 kDa mass (it says 140 residues). Yan et al. is a bioRxiv preprint. Dhavale et al. is solid-state NMR, whose fold matches the cryo-EM one.
