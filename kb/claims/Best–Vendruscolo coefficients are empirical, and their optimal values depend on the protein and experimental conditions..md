## Argument

Bradshaw et al. note that the optimal BV coefficients depend on the protein or experimental conditions. ValDX v1 says they mainly compensate for differences between the experimental conditions and those the model was calibrated under.

## Caveats

Fitted coefficients can therefore absorb forward-model mismatch (revised ValDX, `7_results_omc.tex` L9, L160). Use this narrower claim instead of "systematic errors accumulate at peptide level", whose citations do not support it.
