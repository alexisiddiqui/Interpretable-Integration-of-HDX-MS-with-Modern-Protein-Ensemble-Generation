## Argument

In the classical limit the charge transition dynamics of an oxide defect are determined by the crossing point of the diabatic potential-energy curves ([[@waldhoerMinimumEnergyPaths2019#^c1]]). An applied gate bias shifts the curves relative to each other ([[@waldhoerMinimumEnergyPaths2019#^c2]]). The thesis uses this only as the source of the two-curve picture in panel B of `fig:intro-frustrated-memory`, in the analogy of [[Because folding and unfolding follow distinct pathways at pH 4, the prion landscape can retain the history of the condition applied, which reads as a form of memory.]]

## Caveats

The source is a device-physics paper on amorphous silica and says nothing about proteins.
