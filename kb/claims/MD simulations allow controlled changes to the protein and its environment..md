## Argument

Karplus and McCammon say that a simulation can be used to create a physical system not accessible in nature, such as one with the protein and the solvent at different temperatures, which resolved a question that experiments could not.

## Caveats

The example concerns independent control of protein and solvent temperatures. Karplus and McCammon also describe access to individual particle motions and control over the interaction potentials.
