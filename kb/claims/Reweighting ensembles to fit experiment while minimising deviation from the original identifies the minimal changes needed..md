## Argument

Cesari et al. say the [[maximum entropy principle]] gives, among distributions satisfying the experimental constraints, the one as close as possible to the prior, and Crehuet et al. say maximum entropy produces the minimum perturbation to the original ensemble such that it fits the experimental data.
