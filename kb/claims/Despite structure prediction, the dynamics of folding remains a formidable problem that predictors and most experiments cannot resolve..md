## Argument

Despite AlphaFold's success, the dynamics of protein folding remains a formidable problem ([[@wilsonAlphaFold2RoleDisordered2022#^c1]]). Folding needs atomic resolution at nanosecond timescales, outside the limits of most experimental techniques ([[@laneMillisecondsChallengesSimulation2013#^c1]]), and structure predictors return static, PDB-like structures rather than behaviour in solution ([[@abramsonAccurateStructurePrediction2024#^c6]]). The static half of the folding problem has largely yielded; the dynamic half has not.

## Caveats

Lane et al. predate AlphaFold, so their resolution argument is about experiment and simulation, not prediction. "Largely yielded" applies to single-domain structure prediction, not to complexes or disordered regions.
