## Argument

Masson et al. (2017) state that the intrinsic rate of an open amide is governed by its neighbours, the residue itself, pH and temperature. Vinciauskaite et al. add solvent accessibility and the hydrogen-bonding network. Skinner et al. state that closed hydrogens cannot exchange until a transient opening makes them exchange-competent. Narang et al. state that structural features modulate the observed rate in folded proteins.

## Caveats

The intrinsic-rate parameterisation and its equations belong to Chapter 2 ([[intrinsic exchange rate]]).
