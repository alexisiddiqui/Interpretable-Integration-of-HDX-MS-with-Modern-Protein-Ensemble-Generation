## Argument

Peptide uptake is fixed by its amides' exchange rates, but the rates generally cannot be recovered from it. Fitting each peptide alone leaves residue protection factors severely underdetermined ([[HDX protection-factor model]]).

## Caveats

Overlap, short peptides and more time points reduce the degeneracy (Skinner ^c2); they do not remove it in general.
