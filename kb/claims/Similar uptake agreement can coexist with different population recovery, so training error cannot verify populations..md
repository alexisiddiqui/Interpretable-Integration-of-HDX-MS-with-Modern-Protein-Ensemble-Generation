## Argument

In the TeaA Iso-Validation benchmark, ISO-Bimodal and ISO-Trimodal reached similarly low training error, but ISO-Trimodal's open-state recovery stayed below the 40% target for three of four split types. An ensemble of incorrect structures can reach low training error through the flexibility of the fit.

## Caveats

Synthetic benchmark with exact target populations. The general statement is the paper's conclusion from it ([[overfitting]]).
