## Argument

ValDX reads low validation error together with high Work Done as agreement reached through excessive modification, a signature of overfitting. Experimental agreement alone cannot separate a representative ensemble from one forced into agreement.

## Caveats

A diagnostic pattern, not a test with a threshold. High Work Done shows substantial modification with several possible causes, including calibration mismatch and missing states, so on its own it does not establish overfitting (revised ValDX, `projects/1-ValDX/manuscript/report/7_results_omc.tex` L18, L25). Small Work Done can also mean too little fitting ([[Work Done]]).
