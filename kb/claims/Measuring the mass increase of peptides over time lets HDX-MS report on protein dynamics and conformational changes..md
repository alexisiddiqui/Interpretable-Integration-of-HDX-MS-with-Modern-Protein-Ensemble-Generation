## Argument

James et al. state that deuterium incorporation is read out by MS through the roughly one Dalton mass difference, that peptide-level labelling gives a gradual mass shift over deuteration, and that HDX-MS can reveal higher-order structure and dynamics. Masson et al. (2019) describe the measurement as the change in mass from isotopic exchange between backbone amide hydrogens and the solvent.
