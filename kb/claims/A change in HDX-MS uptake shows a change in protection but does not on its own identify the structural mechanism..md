## Argument

Englander and Krishna state that measured exchange does not directly distinguish between alternative structural interpretations, even with residue-resolved data. Masson et al. (2019) attribute the difficulty of interpretation to the complexity of the exchange mechanism. Bradshaw et al. state that HDX data reflect an ensemble that may not be explained by a single state, and that there is no guarantee a peptide set holds enough information for a clear structural interpretation.

## Caveats

Englander's example is native-state HX of cytochrome c measured by NMR, not peptide-level HDX-MS; peptide averaging only adds ambiguity.
