## Argument

Standard statistical learning: bagging and related averaging reduce the variance component of error that comes from the particular sample each fit saw, and the reduction is largest for procedures whose single fits are unstable ([[model averaging]]). The general source, a statistical-learning text, is not yet in the vault; the claim stays draft until it is seeded and quoted.

## Caveats

Variance only. Error that every fit shares is untouched ([[Averaging fitted models reduces their variance but retains any error they share.]]).
