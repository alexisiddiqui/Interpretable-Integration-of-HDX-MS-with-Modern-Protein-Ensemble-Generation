## Argument

James et al. state that HDX-MS applies to nearly any protein system, including many too large, flexible, heterogeneous or sample-limited for other structural tools. Narang et al. call versatility its great advantage, covering large complexes, highly dynamic proteins and membrane-associated species, and note that it needs a fraction of the protein of other techniques. Masson et al. (2017) describe it as a highly sensitive aqueous measurement needing little protein.

## Caveats

This replaces the ValDX wording "without size limitations". James notes elsewhere a practical size limit set by resolving and detecting all peptides.
