## Argument

Generated starting structures do not describe the transitions between them. Sampling the intervening states is needed to characterise those transitions (`milestones/2025-11-confirmation-of-status/introduction/ensemble_generators.tex` L72).

## Caveats

The confirmation manuscript states the sampling requirement. Its Henin citation does not address transitions between structures supplied by learned generators.
