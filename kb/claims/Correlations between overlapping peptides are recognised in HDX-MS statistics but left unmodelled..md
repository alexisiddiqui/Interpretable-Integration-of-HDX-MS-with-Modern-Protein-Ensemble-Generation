## Argument

Crook et al. list, as the first limitation of their functional model, that correlations between overlapping peptides are not modelled ([[@crookEmpiricalBayesFunctional2022#^c1]]), and that this shows as clustered p-values for overlapping peptides ([[@crookEmpiricalBayesFunctional2022#^c2]]) ([[error covariance]]).

## Caveats

One study of differential HDX-MS. Scope the statement to the studies examined.
