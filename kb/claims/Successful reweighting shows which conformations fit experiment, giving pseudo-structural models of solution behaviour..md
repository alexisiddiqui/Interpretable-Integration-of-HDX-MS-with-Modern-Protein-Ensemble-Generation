## Argument

Jia et al. (2023) report that reweighting a mixed XylE ensemble to HDX-MS data clearly separates the structures present in each dataset, and Lee et al. state the aim of deriving objective structural ensembles directly from HDX measurements.

## Caveats

Lee's passage states an aim, not a result. No checked passage says which conformations are inconsistent with experiment, or uses the term pseudo-structural. Jia et al. (2020) has no passage on reweighting.
