## Argument

Grimsley et al. summarise measured pKa values of ionisable groups in folded proteins: their averages are close to the model values, with a spread whose tails come from groups in particular environments ([[@grimsleySummaryMeasuredPK2009#^c1]], [[@grimsleySummaryMeasuredPK2009#^c2]]). The largest shifts come from burial: Lys residues placed in the interior of staphylococcal nuclease titrate several units low ([[@isomLargeShiftsPKa2011#^c1]]). See [[pKa shift]].

## Caveats

The measured set is biased towards groups that are easy to titrate by NMR, and buried groups are under-represented. The Isom variants are engineered, so they show what burial can do, not how often it happens.
