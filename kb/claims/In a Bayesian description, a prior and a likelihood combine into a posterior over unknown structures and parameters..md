## Argument

Habeck's inferential structure determination applies [[Bayes' theorem]] to the joint space of structures and nuisance parameters; the posterior is everything needed to solve the problem. The likelihood comes from a [[forward model]] and an error model. Hamelryck states the general rule: the posterior is proportional to likelihood times prior. See [[inverse problem]].

## Caveats

Habeck infers one structure. For ensemble-averaged HDX data the unknowns are weights and forward-model parameters (inverse problem, step 6).
