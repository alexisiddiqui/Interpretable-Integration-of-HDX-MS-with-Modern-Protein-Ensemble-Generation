## Argument

Native-state hydrogen exchange of cytochrome c shows that the molecule is composed of cooperative units smaller than the whole protein ([[@englanderHydrogenExchangeModern1997#^c6]]), revealed as cooperative substructure spanning the folding pathway ([[@englanderHydrogenExchangeModern1997#^c4]]) ([[cooperative unfolding unit]]).

## Caveats

Residue-resolution NMR on small proteins. Which units are open in a given state can remain ambiguous ([[@englanderHydrogenExchangeModern1997#^c3]]).
