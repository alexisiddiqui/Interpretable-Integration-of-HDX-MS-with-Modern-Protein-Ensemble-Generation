## Argument

Varadi et al. describe AlphaFold as an AI system developed by DeepMind that makes state-of-the-art predictions of protein structures from amino-acid sequences.

## Caveats

Varadi et al. call AlphaFold an AI system developed by DeepMind and do not use the term deep learning. @casadevallAlphaFold2DeepLearning2023 p1 (^c1) calls AF2 a deep-learning algorithm but is not cited for this sentence. Abramson et al. is a likely mis-citation here, since it describes AlphaFold 3 and mentions AF2 only as the model it evolved from.
