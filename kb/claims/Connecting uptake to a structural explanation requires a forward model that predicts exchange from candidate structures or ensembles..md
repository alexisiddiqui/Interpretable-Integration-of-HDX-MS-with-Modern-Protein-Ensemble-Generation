## Argument

Bradshaw et al. call the objective translation of HDX data into structural information the major challenge, and predict uptake from structural snapshots by first computing residue protection factors. Lee et al. state that the link between exchange and structural flexibility makes a structural interpretation possible, and that predicted protection factors are converted to peptide deuteration for direct comparison with HDX-MS. This places structure on the model side: the measurement ends at uptake ([[forward model]], [[inverse problem]]).
