## Argument

Yang et al. state that although a protein's sequence is foretold in the genome, its conformation is sculpted by life history, and that prions can encode and transmit an epigenetic record of that history between cells and individuals ([[@yangMolecularMechanismEnvironmental2026#^c1]]). See [[prion protein]].

## Caveats

The source is a preprint that has not been peer reviewed. The statement is the paper's framing of its own system, the yeast non-amyloid prion [SMAUG+].
