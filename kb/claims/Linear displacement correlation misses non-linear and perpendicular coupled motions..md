## Argument

Pearson correlation of displacements does not account for non-linear contributions and fails for correlated motion in perpendicular directions ([[@meloGeneralizedCorrelationbasedDynamical2020#^c2]]). Dynamic cross-correlation is this linear coefficient applied to displacement vectors ([[positional covariance]]), so a near-zero value does not show that two regions move independently.

## Caveats

Generalised correlation based on mutual information avoids this limit at higher cost ([[@meloGeneralizedCorrelationbasedDynamical2020#^c3]]). DCC also assumes one mean position per atom, a separate limit for multi-modal motion ([[@kasaharaNovelApproachDynamic2014#^c3]]).
