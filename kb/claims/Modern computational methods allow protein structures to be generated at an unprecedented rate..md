## Argument

Zheng et al. describe AlphaFold and RoseTTAFold as end-to-end deep learning methods able to generate high-quality structures for entire proteomes, and say such tools have drastically expanded the structural coverage of proteins. Abramson et al. report that AlphaFold 3 predicts complexes of nearly all molecular types in the PDB with high accuracy.

## Caveats

Neither paper quantifies a rate; the support is for the scale and accuracy of structure generation, not for the word 'unprecedented'.
