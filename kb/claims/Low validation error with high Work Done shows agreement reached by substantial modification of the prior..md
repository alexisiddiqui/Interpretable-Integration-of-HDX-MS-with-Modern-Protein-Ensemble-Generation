## Argument

ValDX reads low validation error together with high Work Done as agreement reached through substantial modification of the prior. The pattern is consistent with overfitting, but it can also arise from a poor prior or from mismatch in the forward model, so the structural hypothesis is treated with caution in proportion to the spread of metrics across replicate optimisations (revised `projects/1-ValDX/manuscript/report/7_results_omc.tex` L25). Experimental agreement alone cannot separate a representative ensemble from one forced into agreement.

## Caveats

A diagnostic pattern, not a test with a threshold. High Work Done shows substantial modification with several possible causes, including calibration mismatch and missing states, so on its own it does not establish overfitting (revised ValDX, `projects/1-ValDX/manuscript/report/7_results_omc.tex` L18, L25). Posted v1 reads the pattern as excessive modification and a signature of overfitting ([[@siddiquiFrameworkTestingStructural2026#^c11]]); the revised reading is used. Small Work Done can also mean too little fitting ([[Work Done]]).
