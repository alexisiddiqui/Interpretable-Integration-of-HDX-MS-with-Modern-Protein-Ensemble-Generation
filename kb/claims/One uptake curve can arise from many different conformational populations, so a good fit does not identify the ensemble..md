## Argument

Uptake is averaged over each peptide, so one curve has many structural explanations (ValDX v1, p. 2). More generally the ensemble has far more degrees of freedom than there are independent observables (Berlin et al.), and there is no guarantee that a set of peptides holds enough information for a clear structural interpretation (Bradshaw et al.).

## Caveats

The revised ValDX manuscript adds that even a perfect forward model leaves protection factors underdetermined (`1_introduction_omc.tex` L17). That sentence is not in posted v1 and has no citation, so keep it as the thesis's own statement until a revised version is posted.
