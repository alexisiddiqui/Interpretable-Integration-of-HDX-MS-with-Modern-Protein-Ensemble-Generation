## Argument

Yim et al. describe generation by progressively modifying noisy samples towards clean examples ([[@yimDiffusionModelsProtein2024#^c2]]). They report state-of-the-art results in protein 3D structure generation and small molecule docking ([[@yimDiffusionModelsProtein2024#^c1]]).

## Caveats

The review covers structure generation, design and docking. These applications are not all structure-prediction tasks.
