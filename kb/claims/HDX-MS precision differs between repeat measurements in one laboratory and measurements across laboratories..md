## Argument

Precision is characterised by repeatability, intermediate precision and reproducibility ([[@hudgensInterlaboratoryComparisonHydrogenDeuterium2019#^c1]]); reproducibility lets instruments, reagents, locations and operators vary ([[@hudgensInterlaboratoryComparisonHydrogenDeuterium2019#^c2]]). For bottom-up HDX-MS the interlaboratory reproducibility of corrected uptake was (9.0 ± 0.9) % ([[@hudgensInterlaboratoryComparisonHydrogenDeuterium2019#^c3]]) ([[experimental uncertainty]]).

## Caveats

One reference protein under a shared protocol. The numbers describe corrected peptide uptake, not quantities inferred from it.
