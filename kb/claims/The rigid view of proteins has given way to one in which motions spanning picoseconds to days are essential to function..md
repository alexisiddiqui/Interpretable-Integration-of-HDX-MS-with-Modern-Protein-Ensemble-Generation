## Argument

Karplus and Kuriyan describe the replacement of the rigid view of proteins by a dynamic one in which internal motions are essential to function ([[@karplusMolecularDynamicsSimulations2002#^c4]]). Those motions span picoseconds to days ([[@sapienzaUsingNMRStudy2010#^c2]]), and conformational changes cover a broad range of timescales ([[@klecknerIntroductionNMRbasedApproaches2011#^c2]]). The range matters for what follows: different methods see different parts of it ([[protein dynamics]]).

## Caveats

The picosecond-to-days range is given in the context of what NMR can access. It describes motions in general, not the timescale of any particular function.
