## Argument

Kleckner and Foster present eight [[NMR]] methods for protein dynamics, spanning a broad range of timescales, within a common experimental and analytical framework.

## Caveats

The quoted passages support the broad timescale range and the common framework. They do not state the picosecond-to-second range for nuclear spin relaxation specifically; the paper places spin relaxation at ps-ns and treats other methods (e.g. relaxation dispersion, exchange spectroscopy) at slower timescales.
