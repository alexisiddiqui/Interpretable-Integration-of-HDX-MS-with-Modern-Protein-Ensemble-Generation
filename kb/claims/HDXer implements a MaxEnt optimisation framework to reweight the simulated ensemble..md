## Argument

Lee et al. classify HDXer as a post-hoc maximum entropy ensemble reweighting method, and Cesari et al. review maximum entropy methods implemented as reweighting of existing simulations.

## Caveats

Cesari et al. describe the general [[maximum entropy principle]] framework and do not mention HDXer.
