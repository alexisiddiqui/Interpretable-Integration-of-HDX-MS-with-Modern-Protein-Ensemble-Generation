## Argument

Lee et al. say the link between deuterium exchange and structural flexibility makes it possible to interpret [[HDX-MS]] data in a structural context, and that next-generation HDX studies need quantitative, objective structural ensembles derived from the measurements. Best and Vendruscolo describe protection factors as an important source of structural information.

## Caveats

Neither paper discusses biological mechanisms or says that structural interpretation is required to connect dynamics measurements to them; the passages show that structural interpretation of exchange data is the aim, not that it is a necessary step. Bradshaw et al. (p22, not cited here) state this more directly: the challenge is to translate HDX data into structural information so as to derive quantitative mechanistic insights.
