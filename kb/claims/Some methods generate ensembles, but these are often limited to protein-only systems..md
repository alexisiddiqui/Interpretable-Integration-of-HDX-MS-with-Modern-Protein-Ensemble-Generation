## Argument

Casadevall et al. describe MSA-depth reduction as a way to generate multiple AF2 conformations, and say AF2-type methods do not predict non-protein parts such as cofactors, substrates and metal ions.

## Caveats

The protein-only limitation is stated for AF2-type methods in general, not specifically for the ensemble-generating methods.
