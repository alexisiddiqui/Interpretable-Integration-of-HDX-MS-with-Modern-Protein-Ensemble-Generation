## Argument

In a large survey of rare human coding variants, disease-related single-nucleotide variants changed local frustration more than non-disease variants ([[@parraFrustrationPhysiologyMolecular2025#^c23]]). This gives the case studies in §1.1 group D a population-level backing: perturbing the local energetics that set state populations is a common feature of pathogenic variants.

## Caveats

Reported second-hand in a review; the primary source is Kumar et al. (2016), which should be imported and cited. A difference in frustration change is correlational and does not show mechanism for any one variant.
