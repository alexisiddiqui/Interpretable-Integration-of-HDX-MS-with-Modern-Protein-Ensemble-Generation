## Argument

Maveyraud and Mourey name X-ray crystallography, NMR and cryo-EM as the main structure-determination tools, and James et al. describe them as giving exact atomic positions and cryo-EM as having advanced. Bai et al. say cryo-EM resolution is now beginning to rival X-ray crystallography, thanks to recent advances in electron detection and image processing.

## Caveats

No checked passage says these techniques give 'static' structures or that the progress is 'tremendous'. Masson (p2) lists X-ray crystallography, NMR and electron microscopy as complementary high-resolution techniques and (p9) refers to dynamic processes invisible to cryo-EM and X-ray, but does not say they made tremendous strides in static structures.
