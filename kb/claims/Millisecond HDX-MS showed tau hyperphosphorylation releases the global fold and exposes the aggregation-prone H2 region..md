## Argument

Narang et al. review millisecond [[HDX-MS]] on tau: the native ensemble has a global fold with the hexapeptides sequestered, whereas the hyperphosphorylated ensemble releases the N- and C-termini and fully exposes H2.

## Caveats

The sentence adds that this drives amyloidogenic transitions, and the title drops that causal wording. Narang et al. say the hexapeptide motifs have been linked to amyloidogenic propensity and that hyperphosphorylated tau is more amyloidogenic.
