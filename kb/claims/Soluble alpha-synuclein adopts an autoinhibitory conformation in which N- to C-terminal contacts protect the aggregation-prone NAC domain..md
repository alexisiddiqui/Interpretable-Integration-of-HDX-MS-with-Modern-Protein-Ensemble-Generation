## Argument

Monomeric [[alpha-synuclein]] is disordered and populates multiple conformational states ([[@ahmedRefinementASynucleinEnsembles2021#^c5]]). Within that ensemble, an electrostatic interaction between the N- and C-termini gives an autoinhibitory conformation that protects the aggregation-prone NAC domain ([[@mukherjeeProteinMisfoldingAmyloid2024#^c6]]). Protection is a property of the ensemble's populations, not of a single structure.

## Caveats

This replaces the unsupported "compact through C-terminus–hydrophobic centre contacts" wording; Mukherjee describes an N- to C-terminal contact instead.
