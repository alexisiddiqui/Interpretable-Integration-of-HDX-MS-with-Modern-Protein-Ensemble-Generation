## Argument

Pelikan et al. say [[SAXS]] yields only size, shape and conformational distribution, that data quality from low-concentration samples has improved, and that it can give information on flexible or disordered molecules. Ahmed et al. say integrating several biophysical experiments is often necessary to characterise IDP structure and dynamics.
