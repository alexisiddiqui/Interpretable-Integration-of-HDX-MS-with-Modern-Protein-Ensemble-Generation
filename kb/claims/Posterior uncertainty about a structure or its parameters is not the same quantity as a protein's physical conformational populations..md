## Argument

Habeck: a probability quantifies ignorance about a parameter and does not relate to a physical propensity or fluctuation. An NMR ensemble is not an experimentally determined thermodynamic ensemble, and statements that it reflects dynamics do not follow from how the data were modelled. See [[posterior uncertainty]].

## Caveats

Habeck writes about single-structure NMR calculations. For ensemble-averaged data the physical populations are the weights, and posterior uncertainty sits on top of them.
