## Argument

jaxENT's averaging-mode baseline records, for the synthetic TeaA benchmark, that rate- and uptake-averaged predictions fit held-out uptake better than log-protection-factor averaging while the log-protection-factor candidates recovered the populations better (pinned lines). What is averaged over frames is therefore a forward-model choice that validation and recovery can rank differently ([[forward model]], [[model misspecification]], [[Evaluation design (jaxENT)]]).

## Caveats

Project document only, not a manuscript result. Numbers are a soft-regression baseline, not a benchmark.
