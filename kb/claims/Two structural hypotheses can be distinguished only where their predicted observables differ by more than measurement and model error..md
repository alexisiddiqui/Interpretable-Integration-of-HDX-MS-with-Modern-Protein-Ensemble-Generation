## Argument

One uptake curve has many structural explanations ([[@siddiquiFrameworkTestingStructural2026#^c1]]), and a peptide set may not hold enough information for a clear interpretation ([[@bradshawInterpretationHDXData2020#^c8]]). The working consequence is that a structural difference matters to the data only through the observables it changes. Where two hypotheses predict uptake within the combined measurement and forward-model error, no fit to that uptake can prefer one ([[identifiability]]).

## Caveats

The error budget includes forward-model error, which is itself uncertain ([[model misspecification]]). An orthogonal measurement can separate hypotheses that uptake cannot, but only if it is sensitive to the distinction that uptake leaves open.
