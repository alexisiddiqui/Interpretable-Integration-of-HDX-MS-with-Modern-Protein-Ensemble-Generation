## Argument

Yim et al. review diffusion models and say they have achieved state-of-the-art results in protein 3D structure generation and small molecule docking.

## Caveats

Yim et al. (p1 ^c1 and p2) concern structure generation, design and docking with diffusion models; they do not state that diffusion models are an alternative to structure prediction methods.
