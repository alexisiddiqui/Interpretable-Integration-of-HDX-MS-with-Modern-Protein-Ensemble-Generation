## Argument

The Best–Vendruscolo coefficients depend on protein and conditions ([[@bradshawInterpretationHDXData2020#^c13]]) and mainly compensate for condition differences ([[@siddiquiFrameworkTestingStructural2026#^c7]]), so recalibration is the first response to a calibration pattern ([[@siddiquiFrameworkTestingStructural2026#^c15]]). Under imposed kinetic mismatch, recalibration restored uptake agreement without recovering the populations (revised ValDX, L248), which is the signature of an inadequate form ([[model misspecification]]).
