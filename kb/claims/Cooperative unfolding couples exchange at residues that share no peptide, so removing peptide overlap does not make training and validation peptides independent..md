## Argument

Residues open in cooperative units smaller than the protein ([[@englanderHydrogenExchangeModern1997#^c6]]), and a unit can join segments far apart in sequence ([[@englanderHydrogenExchangeModern1997#^c7]]); in MoPrP whole segments exchange through subglobal unfolding ([[@moulickPartiallyUnfoldedForms2015#^c1]]). Two peptides from different regions can therefore report the same opening. ValDX states that residue-wise observations without overlap may remain correlated through shared structural environments (`6_methods_omc.tex` L52) ([[cooperative unfolding unit]], [[data splitting]]).

## Caveats

The coupling is thermodynamic under EX2 and its unit assignment can be ambiguous. The consequence for splits is ours; the sources establish the units.
