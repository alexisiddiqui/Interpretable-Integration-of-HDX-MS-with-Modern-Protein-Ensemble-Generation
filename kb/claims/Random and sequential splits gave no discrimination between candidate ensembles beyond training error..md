## Argument

On experimental peptide datasets, Random and Sequential splits provided no additional discriminatory power beyond training error, so all later experiments use only the Non-Redundant and Spatial splits ([[@siddiquiFrameworkTestingStructural2026#^c16]]) ([[data splitting]], [[model selection]]).

## Caveats

A result about discrimination between ensembles, not a measurement of leakage itself.
