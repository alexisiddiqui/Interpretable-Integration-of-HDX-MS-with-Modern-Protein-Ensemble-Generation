## Argument

The confirmation manuscript identifies the limitation of using static structures to explain the relationships between protein structure, dynamics, function and disease (`milestones/2025-11-confirmation-of-status/introduction/biological_dynamics.tex` L15). Amaro et al. describe the dynamic nature of drug targets and the complexity of biological systems as challenges for understanding their behaviour ([[@amaroMultiscaleMethodsDrug2018#^c1]]).

## Caveats

This limitation concerns explanations based on static structures alone. Experimental methods differ in which motions and timescales they can resolve.
