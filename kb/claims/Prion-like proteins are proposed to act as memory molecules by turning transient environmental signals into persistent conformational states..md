## Argument

Molecular memory is the conversion of transient environmental signals into persistent molecular states ([[@johnsonPrionlikeProteinsFacilitators2026#^c1]]). Johnson and Meyer speculate that prion-like proteins may function as memory molecules, through switch-like changes in conformation or assembly state ([[@johnsonPrionlikeProteinsFacilitators2026#^c2]]). See [[prion protein]].

## Caveats

An opinion piece about plants. The authors present the proposal as speculation, so the claim states it as proposed.
