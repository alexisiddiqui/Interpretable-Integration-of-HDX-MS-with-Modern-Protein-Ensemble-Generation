## Argument

In the Gaussian noise model each datum has an effective uncertainty combining finite-replica error, experimental and systematic error, and forward-model error ([[@bonomiMetadynamicMetainferenceEnhanced2016#^c4]]). One inferred scalar cannot be decomposed back into its contributions ([[metainference]], [[experimental uncertainty]], [[nuisance parameter]]).

## Caveats

The first half is stated by the source; the second is ours.
