## Argument

Uptake is set by the protection factor ([[protection factor]]), the rate expected for a freely exposed hydrogen divided by the measured rate (Skinner et al.), which quantifies how far secondary and tertiary structure slow exchange (Smith et al.). Masson et al. (2017) state that the exact molecular reasons an amide exchanges at a given rate are still not clear, and Masson et al. (2019) warn that the simple experiment is not simple to interpret because the exchange mechanism is complex. Reading uptake as "flexibility" therefore adds an interpretation the measurement does not make (step ours).

## Caveats

The origin sentence ("more exposed and flexible regions exchanging more rapidly") is supported as stated; this claim narrows how it should be read. The definition of the protection factor supports the first sentence, so the only inferred step is the contrast with flexibility.
