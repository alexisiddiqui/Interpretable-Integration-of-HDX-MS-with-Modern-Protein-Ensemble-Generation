## Argument

Uptake is set by the protection factor, the ratio of intrinsic to observed rate ([[protection factor]]). Masson et al. (2017) state that the exact molecular reasons an amide exchanges at a given rate are still not clear, and Masson et al. (2019) warn that the simple experiment is not simple to interpret because the exchange mechanism is complex. Reading uptake as "flexibility" therefore adds an interpretation the measurement does not make (step ours).

## Caveats

The origin sentence ("more exposed and flexible regions exchanging more rapidly") is supported as stated; this claim narrows how it should be read.
