## Argument

ValDX finds optimisation outcomes depend strongly on the peptides and time points used for fitting, and uses replicate splits to quantify it ([[replicate uncertainty]]).

## Caveats

Replicate spread is sensitivity to data selection; it is neither posterior uncertainty nor physical heterogeneity ([[posterior uncertainty]]).
