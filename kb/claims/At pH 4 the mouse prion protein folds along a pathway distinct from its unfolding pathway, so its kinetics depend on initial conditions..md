## Argument

Folding and unfolding of the mouse prion protein use the same single pathway at pH 7, but at pH 4 folding occurs on a pathway distinct from the unfolding pathway, and the kinetics at pH 4 also depend on the initial conditions ([[@moulickRuggednessFreeEnergy2019#^c1]]). The denaturant used to drive the transitions is urea (panel A of `fig:intro-frustrated-memory`). See [[prion protein]] and [[conformational free-energy landscape]].

## Caveats

The study uses the C-terminal domain of the mouse prion protein under urea denaturation, so pH and urea act together here.
