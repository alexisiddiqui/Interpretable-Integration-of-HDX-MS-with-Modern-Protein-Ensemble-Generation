## Argument

Lee et al. describe HDXer as reweighting a simulated candidate ensemble to match exchange data and say next-generation HDX studies must derive objective ensembles directly from measurements; Bradshaw et al. describe the same approach, generating an ensemble by MD and reweighting it to HDX-MS data.

## Caveats

Both papers present one integrative method for HDX-MS; neither argues in general that bridging the gap requires a paradigm shift to integrative approaches.
