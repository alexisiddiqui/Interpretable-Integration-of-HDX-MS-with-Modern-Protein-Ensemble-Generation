## Argument

In PKA, a single Tyr mutation has minimal effect on NMR chemical shifts, the crystal structure and nucleotide binding, but strongly reduces substrate affinity ([[@lisiAllosteryEnzymeCatalysis2017#^c6]]).

## Caveats

PKA is not a disease example. If a disease case is wanted, p53 needs a primary source showing near-identical mutant crystal structures; none is in the KB.
