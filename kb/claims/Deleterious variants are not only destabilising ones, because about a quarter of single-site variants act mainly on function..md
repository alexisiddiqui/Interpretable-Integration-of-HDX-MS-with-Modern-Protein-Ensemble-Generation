## Argument

Most single-site variants of a natural sequence are deleterious, and often the impact is due to destabilisation of the native structure ([[@galpernProbingDarkEnergy2026#^c14]]). The outliers from this trend are functionally deleterious mutations that do not proportionally affect folding stability ([[@galpernProbingDarkEnergy2026#^c15]]), and a two-component mixture puts about a quarter of single-site variants in the mainly function-related group ([[@galpernProbingDarkEnergy2026#^c5]]). This is the content of `fig:intro-destabilising-mutations`. See [[dark energy]] and [[dynamic disease mechanism]].

## Caveats

"About a quarter" depends on the Gaussian-mixture threshold (25.3% here) and on the folding-energy method, and the paper's range across methods is 25 to 35%.
