## Argument

Metainference gives each datum an effective uncertainty combining finite-replica, experimental, systematic and forward-model error, sampled with the structures. With errors present the restraint strength is modulated by the inferred uncertainty ([[metainference]], [[nuisance parameter]]).

## Caveats

A large inferred uncertainty protects against noise but does not say which error source is present.
