## Argument

Abramson et al. state that structure prediction models typically predict static structures as seen in the PDB, not dynamical behaviour in solution, and that for AF3 multiple random seeds for the diffusion head or the whole network do not approximate the solution ensemble.

## Caveats

Replaces the confirmation's broader wording that no approaches generate conformational ensembles. AF3 still gives "a distribution of answers" ([[@abramsonAccurateStructurePrediction2024#^c5]]); it is not a population estimate.
