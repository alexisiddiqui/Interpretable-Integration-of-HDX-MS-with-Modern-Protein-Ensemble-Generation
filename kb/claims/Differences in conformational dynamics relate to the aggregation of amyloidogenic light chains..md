## Argument

Paissoni et al. summarise earlier work showing that differences in conformational dynamics can play a role in the aggregation of amyloidogenic antibody light chains ([[@paissoniConformationalFingerprintAmyloidogenic2025#^c1]]). It extends the [[protein misfolding and aggregation|misfolding]] case beyond the neurodegenerative proteins to a folded domain whose dynamics, not its fold, distinguishes pathogenic variants.

## Caveats

The statement summarises prior work in the paper's introduction; the specific light-chain studies should be cited directly if the example is used.
