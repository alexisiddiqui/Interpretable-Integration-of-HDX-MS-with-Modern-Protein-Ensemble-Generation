## Argument

The revised ValDX manuscript says the framework measures the distance from the prior ensemble to the fitted ensemble, not the distance to a ground truth, and that small values alone can indicate lack of fitting (`7_results_omc.tex` L12, L83; `6_methods_omc.tex` L510).

## Caveats

Posted v1 reads small adjustments as a sign that the ensemble was already representative ([[@siddiquiFrameworkTestingStructural2026#^c10]]). Use the revised wording; upgrade when a revised preprint is posted.
