## Argument

Soto and Pritzkow describe misfolded oligomers as an ill-defined, heterogeneous group of species, and say that their heterogeneity and rapid interconversion have made high-resolution structural information very difficult to obtain. They also say the absence of high-resolution structural information for PrPSc has limited understanding of prion strains. Both are cases where structural biology has been limited.

## Caveats

Soto and Pritzkow make these points about misfolded oligomers and PrPSc specifically; the sentence generalizes to protein-misfolding diseases. The other cited paper (Mukherjee) could not be checked. The Biancaniello paper (@biancanielloStructureDynamicsMisfolding2025 p1 ^c2, p8 ^c9) makes a similar point about X-ray crystallography, NMR and cryo-EM, but it is not cited for this sentence.
