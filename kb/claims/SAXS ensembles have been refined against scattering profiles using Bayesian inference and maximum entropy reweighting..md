## Argument

Chatzimagas and Hub describe [[SAXS]]-driven ensemble refinement committed to the [[maximum entropy principle]] and a Bayesian formulation of SAXS-driven structure refinement.

## Caveats

The paper describes SAXS-driven parallel-replica MD ensemble refinement under the maximum entropy principle, not post hoc reweighting of an existing ensemble, so 'reweighting' is a looser term than the paper uses.
