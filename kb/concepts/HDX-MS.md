## Definition

HDX-MS measures the exchange of backbone amide hydrogens with deuterium in the solvent, and so reports on protein structure and conformational dynamics in solution. Its benefits are sensitivity, flexibility and low sample needs, but interpreting the data in structural terms is hard because of limited spatial resolution and ensemble averaging.

## Why it matters here

HDX-MS is the experimental measurement that this thesis integrates with protein ensemble generation (ValDX and JaxENT), so the difficulty of interpreting it in structural terms is the problem the thesis addresses.
