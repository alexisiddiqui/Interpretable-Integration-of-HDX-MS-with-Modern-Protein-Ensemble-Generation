## Definition

A Boltzmann generator is an invertible neural network trained to map samples from a simple distribution to configurations that are probable under the [[Boltzmann distribution]], so equilibrium-like samples come out in one shot rather than through many small MD steps ([[@noeBoltzmannGeneratorsSampling2019#^c1]], [[@noeBoltzmannGeneratorsSampling2019#^c2]]). Because the network's own distribution is only close to the target, the samples must be reweighted to the Boltzmann distribution before computing thermodynamics ([[@noeBoltzmannGeneratorsSampling2019#^c3]]). That reweighting is [[importance sampling]] with the generator as proposal.

## Equations

$$ w(x) \propto \frac{e^{-U(x)/k_BT}}{P_0(x)}, \qquad x \sim P_0 $$
^eq-bg-weight

Source: [[@noeBoltzmannGeneratorsSampling2019#^c3]], p. 3, where Noé et al. write $w(x) = e^{-u(x)}/p_X(x)$ with the reduced energy $u = U/k_BT$ and the generated density $p_X$. Here $P_0$ is the generator's distribution, as in [[importance sampling#^eq-importance-sampling]].

## Assumptions and limits

- The weights need the generator's exact density $P_0(x)$, which an invertible network provides. Generators without a tractable density (most diffusion and structure-prediction models) cannot be reweighted this way (ours).
- Reweighting works only where the generator overlaps the Boltzmann distribution; few dominant weights mean poor overlap ([[importance sampling]]).
- Not a [[force field]]. A machine-learned force field learns energies and forces for MD ([[@unkeBiomolecularDynamicsMachinelearned2024#^c1]]); a Boltzmann generator samples configurations given an energy. The confirmation cites them as one idea.
- Noé et al. demonstrate small proteins and model systems; this note uses the idea, not a claim about scale.

## Why it matters here

It is the clearest case of a learned generator whose output becomes a population estimate only through statistical reweighting. ValDX and jaxENT do the same with experiment in place of the energy: the generator or simulation supplies $P_0$, and HDX-MS supplies the target ([[ensemble reweighting]]).

Claims that use this term: [[Machine-learned force fields learn energies and forces for simulation, whereas Boltzmann generators generate configurations and reweight them to the Boltzmann distribution.]], [[Learning the physical landscape can reduce the cost of sampling new states while retaining the accuracy of the underlying physics.]], [[Ensembles can be generated by learning the physical landscape of protein systems directly from existing simulations.]].
